## Description
In 1962, a group of West Berlin students dug a tunnel from a factory cellar on Bernauer Straße, under the Berlin Wall, into East Berlin. Behind them crawled an American TV crew. NBC had paid for the story.
This is the story of Tunnel 29: the diggers, the flood that nearly killed the project, the Stasi trap set for a rival tunnel, the night 29 people crawled to freedom, and why the film almost never aired.
