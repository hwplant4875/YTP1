## Patterns shared by all 4

1. **Title formula:** "Ranking [adjective] [topic] Moments", in a black (or near-black) bar across the top that never changes. Two of the four highlight the adjective and topic words in yellow/blue/red, with the rest in white.
2. **Numbered list on the left edge, visible from frame 0** with empty slots. Each slot fills in with a label when its clip plays, and filled labels stay on screen. Viewers watch to see the list complete, which is the retention hook.
3. **Multi-coloured numbers** (yellow/red/blue/green/orange) with heavy outline or glow, each slot a different colour, and white labels.
4. **Labels are 1–5 word punchlines** ("Nice assist🍑", "-1000 aura☠️", "Power Off", "gravity won in 2 seconds") or just "Level N". There is no explanation; the joke is in the label.
5. **#1 is always last.** The order is a countdown (6→1 or 5→1) or a shuffled middle (3→4→5→2→1) that always ends on slot 1, the biggest fail.
6. **Clip count and length:** 5–6 clips. Short videos give each clip ~2.5–5 s (19–21 s total); the long one gives each 5–12 s (47 s total). The cut always lands right after the impact.
7. **Frame 0 already has motion or tension:** the person is mid-exercise and the fail is seconds away. No intro, no logo, no talking.
8. **All sources are reposted user phone clips,** mostly vertical. Horizontal ones are letterboxed, either over a blurred copy (#1) or with black bars (#3). Some keep the original creator's TikTok caption. No channel watermark is visible on any video, and only #2 has a "do not imitate / no one was harmed" disclaimer.
9. **Audio is the original clip sound,** with no voiceover and no single added music track. Loudness varies from clip to clip.
10. **Editing is minimal:** hard cuts. The only effects seen were a typewriter label reveal and one zoom-in replay (#1). No slow-mo, freeze frames, flashes, sound effects or transitions.
11. **No end card and no CTA.** Each video hard-cuts on the #1 fail with the full list showing, which encourages replays and loops.
