# You, Elsewhere EP4 "Christmas Eve 2009": 30-min jazz piano trio Christmas suite

Base: the EP3 library suite pipeline and sound (you_elsewhere/ep03_malfoid_library/music/compose_library_suite.py):
Salamander piano warm chain, upright bass full and present (never empty), NO ride, soft brushes only,
first chorus of each carol = melody exactly as written and instantly recognisable, chords always contain the
melody note (auto-drop clashing comp notes), variations only after the first chorus. Original stereo, steady
energy (LRA ~2-3), -17 LUFS, 4 s crossfades.

Christmas colour (subtle): celesta doubling on some first choruses (same register as the melody, quieter),
very soft sleigh-bell shaker on 1-2 up-tempo carols only. Warm, cozy, nostalgic snowy-night mood.

Repertoire: PUBLIC DOMAIN melodies only (traditional / composer died long ago). Write melodies from reliable
public-domain sources and double-check each against a published score before rendering:
Silent Night (3/4, opens at 0:00), O Christmas Tree, The First Noel (3/4), Deck the Halls, Jingle Bells,
We Wish You a Merry Christmas (3/4), God Rest Ye Merry Gentlemen, Away in a Manger (3/4), O Holy Night
(Adam 1847), Hark! The Herald Angels Sing, Angels We Have Heard on High, Good King Wenceslas, Jolly Old
Saint Nicholas, Carol of the Bells (Leontovych melody, instrumental only), then Silent Night reprise to close.
NOT allowed: anything 20th-century copyrighted (White Christmas, Let It Snow, Have Yourself..., Christmas Song,
Winter Wonderland, Rudolph, Frosty, Jingle Bell Rock, Sleigh Ride (Anderson), Linus and Lucy, etc.).

Tempos: relaxed but not slow, ~92-118 BPM depending on the carol; no slow ballads.
Length ~30:00, ~12-13 tracks x ~2:20.
