## Procedural (built in voldemort_room.py / make_portrait.py, no external asset)
Room shell, chimney breast, marble surround geometry and arched cast-iron insert, grate, volumetric
flames, embers and ash, wingback armchair, bookcase and all loose books, papers, cloth-simulated dust
sheet, curtains and rod, glass jars, rain-on-glass shader, ivy, cobwebs, haze, dust motes, gravestones,
yew and church silhouettes, gold locket and chain, the faded portrait painting (make_portrait.py),
and (optional `--snake`) Nagini.
