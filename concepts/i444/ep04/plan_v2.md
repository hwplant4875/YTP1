## Every death per film (Wikipedia plot sections + anightmareonelmstreet.fandom.com "List of deaths in the A Nightmare on Elm Street films"; New Nightmare also filmsite.org series-nightmare7)
- **1984:** Tina (dragged up the wall to the ceiling), Rod (bedsheet around his neck in the jail cell, staged as a suicide; the script avoids that word), Glen (pulled into his bed), Marge (sinks into the bed at the climax, pulled through the door window in the last scene). Nancy wins with traps, pulling him into reality, and turning her back on him.
- **Freddy's Revenge:** kills through Jesse's body, mostly in the real world. Coach Schneider (gym showers), Grady, 5 party guests (a 6th is gutted but may survive; a 7th is trampled by other guests, not counted), Kerry (claw through her chest on the bus, last scene). Lisa confesses her love and kisses Freddy, who burns to ash.
- **Dream Warriors:** Phillip (vein puppet, falls), Jennifer (TV), Taryn (syringe fingers), Will (stabbed), Don Thompson (Freddy's skeleton, real world), Nancy (Freddy posing as Don). Survivors Kristen, Kincaid, Joey, Neil. Neil lays the bones to rest with holy water and a crucifix.
- **Dream Master:** Kincaid, Joey (waterbed), Kristen (furnace; passes her power to Alice), then Sheila, Rick, Debbie (cockroach, crushed). Alice uses her friends' powers and shows Freddy his reflection; the souls inside him revolt and are released.
- **Dream Child:** Dan (fused with his motorcycle, hits a truck), Greta (force-fed her own insides, suffocates), Mark (paper, slashed). Freddy uses unborn Jacob as a conduit, so he attacks even while Alice is awake. Amanda Krueger helps; Jacob uses the power Freddy gave him and destroys him.
- **Freddy's Dead:** nearly every child and teen in Springwood already dead. On screen: Carlos (hearing magnified, chalkboard scratch), Spencer (video game, pit), John Doe (the only surviving teen; falls onto bed spikes). Maggie (Katherine Krueger) pulls him into reality, pipe bomb in his chest.
- **New Nightmare:** an ancient entity the films kept trapped, freed when the series ended; "killing her [Heather] will allow it into the real world". Kills FX men Chuck Wilson and Terry Feinstein (opening nightmare; a news report later confirms their bodies were found), Chase (asleep at the wheel), Julie (babysitter, hospital). Abducts Dylan. Heather and Dylan push it into the furnace.
- **Freddy vs. Jason:** Freddy's only own kill is Mark (burned "Freddy's Back"); all 17 other deaths are Jason's. Springwood erased him; anyone who dreams of him is held at Westin Hills on Hypnocil. Freddy, as Pamela, resurrects Jason from Hell. Lori beheads Freddy with Jason's machete.
- **2010:** Dean (diner; cuts his own throat in reality), Kris (bedroom), Jesse (jail cell), Marcus Yeon (webcam video, head smashed into the computer), Gwen (end, through the mirror). Every other kid from the preschool is dead except Nancy and Quentin. Microsleeps. Nancy pulls him into reality and slits his throat. The abuse backstory stays out of the script.
