Title: Every Freddy Krueger Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving Him

Basis (owner 11/18): that film's Freddy is in MY room tonight, coming for me. Odds come from what he does and fails to do in his own film. No "he can't find you" reasons. Order below is the re-check order (waiting for the owner's pick on the card). KILL COUNT cards are a 0 to 10 score (1 kill 1, 3 kills 3, 4 kills 4, 5 to 6 kills 5, 7 to 9 kills 6, 10+ kills 8, a whole town 10).

Stats (STR, INT, KILL, DREAM): FvJ 5 7 1 4 = 95, Revenge 7 3 6 3 = 60, Dream Child 5 7 3 7 = 50, 1984 6 7 4 8 = 35, Dream Warriors 6 8 5 7 = 25, New Nightmare 8 7 4 9 = 20, Freddy's Dead 5 7 10 7 = 15, 2010 7 7 8 8 = 10, Dream Master 8 8 5 10 = 5.

Never show: Dean cutting his own throat, Rod hanging, Phillip's fall, Joey's waterbed, 2010 preschool flashbacks, wrist scenes.

## s00 cold open

He will drag you across the ceiling, turn you into a puppet, smash your head through a TV, crush you like a bug, or pull you down into your own bed. And all he needs is for you to fall asleep. We're ranking every Freddy by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 rules

Here's the rule. You fall asleep tonight, and that movie's Freddy is waiting for you. And every Freddy is different. One is so weak he needs Jason to kill for him, one needs a teenager's body, and one is strong enough to wipe out kids with superpowers. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and dream power, which is how dangerous he gets once your eyes close. Put it all together, and you get one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #9 Freddy vs. Jason (2003), 95%, Easy

Number nine, Freddy versus Jason. This Freddy is the weakest of them all, because his town forgot him. The adults erased every trace of him, and anyone who still dreams about him gets locked away in a hospital and kept on a drug that stops them from dreaming. He's so weak that he has to bring Jason back from Hell to scare people for him. In a movie with his own name on the poster, Jason does nearly all the killing, and apart from an old murder we see in a flashback, the only person Freddy kills himself is Mark. When he comes for Lori, she keeps waking up from him, and in the end she drags him into the real world and cuts his head off with Jason's machete. Even if this one is standing in your dream tonight, you'll almost certainly wake up. Survival rate, ninety-five percent.

## s03 #8 Freddy's Revenge (1985), 60%, Close Call

Number eight, Freddy's Revenge. This Freddy is far more dangerous than the last one, because he doesn't need Jason. He takes over a teenager named Jesse, climbs right out of his body, and does his killing out in the open. He kills Jesse's gym teacher in the showers, then his friend Grady, then several kids at a pool party, and in the very last scene, his claws burst out of Lisa's friend Kerry on the school bus. But he barely uses his dream powers, so he has to come at you in the real world, where you can see him and run, and most of the kids at that party got away. And when he came face to face with Lisa, he couldn't hurt her, because Jesse was still fighting him from the inside, and she followed him into his factory and beat him with a kiss. Everyone he caught alone died, but this is a Freddy you can run from. Survival rate, sixty percent.

## s04 #7 The Dream Child (1989), 50%, Better Run

Number seven, The Dream Child. This Freddy kills fewer people than the last one, but he's far smarter, and he's back inside your dreams. He finds a sneaky new way in, through the dreams of Alice's unborn son, Jacob, so he can come for her friends even while she's wide awake. He fuses Dan to a motorcycle and sends him into oncoming traffic, force-feeds Greta until she chokes, and turns Mark into a paper drawing before slicing him to pieces. But Alice pulled Yvonne out of his dream alive, and she pulled Mark out once too, before Freddy got him the second time. In the end, with help from Freddy's own mother, Jacob uses the power Freddy gave him to destroy him. Half the people he went after lived and half died, so this one is a coin flip. Survival rate, fifty percent.

## s05 #6 A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984), 35%, Almost Dead

Number six, the original Nightmare on Elm Street, from nineteen eighty-four. This Freddy is scarier than the last one, because he barely talks, he stays in the shadows, and nobody believes you until it's too late. He drags Tina across the ceiling, strangles Rod with his own bedsheet in a jail cell, pulls Glen down into his bed, and takes Nancy's mother right in front of her. Of the four teenagers he went after, three died. But Nancy has no powers at all, and she still beats him. She learns his rules, sets traps all over her house, drags him into the real world, and finally turns her back on him, taking away the fear that feeds him. So there is a way to beat this one, but only one kid in four was brave and smart enough to find it. Survival rate, thirty-five percent.

## s06 #5 Dream Warriors (1987), 25%, Slaughtered

Number five, Dream Warriors. This Freddy is deadlier than the last one, because even kids who learn to fight back can't stop him. He hunts the last children of Elm Street in a mental hospital. He turns Phillip into a puppet using his own veins, smashes Jennifer's head into a TV, and kills Taryn and Will even after they find their dream powers. Then his skeleton kills Nancy's father in the real world, and Freddy kills Nancy herself while pretending to be her dad, so even the girl who beat the last Freddy doesn't make it out. Six of the ten people he went after died, and the ones who lived had dream powers and a friend pouring holy water on his bones. You don't have superpowers. Survival rate, twenty-five percent.

## s07 #4 Wes Craven's New Nightmare (1994), 20%, Slaughtered

Number four, New Nightmare. This Freddy is stronger and scarier than the last one, and he doesn't just hunt teenagers, he hunts a whole family. He goes after Heather, the actress who played Nancy, and picks off the people around her one by one. He kills two special effects men working on a new Freddy movie, then her husband while he's driving, then her son's babysitter in a hospital, dragging her up the wall just like Tina. Then he takes her little boy, Dylan, right into his world. Nobody he went for got away from him until Heather followed him into his world and, together with Dylan, pushed him into a furnace. There are no powers and no holy water this time, so the only way out is to walk into his nightmare and win. Survival rate, twenty percent.

## s08 #3 Freddy's Dead (1991), 15%, Slaughtered

Number three, Freddy's Dead. This Freddy is the silliest one of them all, but don't let the jokes fool you, because he has the biggest body count of any Freddy, even if most of it happens before the movie starts. By the time we meet him, he has already killed almost every child and teenager in his town. Then he plays with the few who are left like toys. He turns up Carlos's hearing until one scratch on a chalkboard kills him, traps Spencer inside a video game and kills him there, and drops John Doe, the last teenager from Springwood, onto a bed of spikes. Tracy only lives because Maggie wakes her up in time, and it takes Maggie, his own daughter, dragging him into the real world and shoving a pipe bomb into his chest to end him. A whole town of kids couldn't survive this Freddy, so unless someone wakes you up in time, you won't either. Survival rate, fifteen percent.

## s09 #2 the 2010 remake, 10%, Slaughtered

Number two, the twenty ten remake. This Freddy is the opposite of the last one. He's dark, mean, and serious, and he has a trick that makes staying awake useless. Go too long without sleep, and your brain drops into his world for a few seconds at a time, without you even noticing. By the end, every other kid from Nancy's old preschool class is dead except her and Quentin. He gets Dean in a diner, Kris in her bedroom, and Jesse in a jail cell, and even after Nancy drags him into the real world and cuts his throat, he comes back through a mirror for her mother. Out of that whole class, only two kids lived, and they had to pump themselves full of adrenaline and fight him together. Survival rate, ten percent.

## s10 #1 The Dream Master (1988), 5%, Slaughtered

And number one, The Dream Master. This Freddy is the strongest of them all, the one who wiped out the Dream Warriors. Kincaid, Joey and Kristen, the kids with dream powers who beat him in Dream Warriors, are all dead early in the movie. Then he goes through Alice's friends one by one, Sheila, Rick, and Debbie, who he turns into a cockroach and crushes, and every soul he takes stays trapped inside him. Alice only wins by taking on her dead friends' powers and showing Freddy his own reflection, so the souls inside him break free and tear him apart. Even the kids with superpowers died, and the only one who beat him had to take all of their powers first. You don't stand a chance. Survival rate, five percent.
