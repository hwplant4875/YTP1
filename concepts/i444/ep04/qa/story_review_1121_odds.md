## Counting rule for KILL COUNT

A kill counts only if this film's Freddy personally causes it during the film's present story (dream or real world, including through a possessed host). Kills by Jason, accidents like trampling, and pre-film flashback murders are excluded. "On-screen" = death shown or a body shown right after. "Implied" = the film states deaths that are not shown. Stat scale: 1 kill = 1, 3 = 3, 4 = 4, 5 to 6 = 5, 7 to 9 = 6, 10 to 15 = 8, a whole town = 10.

## Per film breakdown

### A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984)
- Targets: Tina Gray, Rod Lane, Glen Lantz, Nancy Thompson, Marge Thompson.
- Died: Tina (dream, slashed, dragged up wall and ceiling; real body slashed). Rod (hanged with a sheet in his cell, staged as suicide). Glen (pulled into his bed, blood geyser). Marge (grabbed through the door window in the final scene; ambiguous dream ending; fandom lists her with "either strangled/burned or pulled through window").
- Survived a direct attack: Nancy, repeatedly. Burned her arm on a pipe to wake herself, alarm clock woke her, caffeine, a watcher (Glen), pulled his hat out of the dream, then pulled Freddy himself into the real world, booby trapped the house (sledgehammer, trip wire, fire), and finally turned her back on him, taking her fear away, and he evaporated.
- How hard: very hard but done by an ordinary teenager with household tools and her dad (a cop) nearby.
- Share of direct targets killed: 3 of 5 confirmed (60%), 4 of 5 if Marge counts.
- Weaknesses: fueled by fear (turning your back dissolves him), can be pulled into the real world where traps and fire hurt him, can be woken out of, objects come back from dreams.
- Raw kills: on-screen 3 (Tina, Rod, Glen) plus 1 ambiguous (Marge). Implied 0.

### Freddy's Revenge (1985)
- Targets: Jesse Walsh (host), Coach Schneider, Ron Grady, Lisa Webber, guests at Lisa's pool party, Kerry (ending).
- Died: Schneider (real world, gym showers, slashed). Grady (real world, Freddy emerges from Jesse's body). Party guests (real world, slashed, boiled in the pool, burned, thrown through a grill). Fandom list gives 6 guests killed by Freddy plus 1 trampled by the crowd; other fan sources say 6 or 7. Kerry (claw through chest on the bus in the ending, which may be a dream).
- Survived a direct attack: Lisa. Freddy "realizes he cannot harm her due to Jesse's influence" (Wikipedia). She controlled her fear, confessed her love, kissed him, and Jesse fought back from inside. Most of the party crowd fled and lived.
- How hard: needed one person who loved the host and was not afraid.
- Share killed of those he struck at: about 9 of 11, but out of a party of dozens most escaped.
- Weaknesses: needs a host body and works only through Jesse; Jesse's terror gives him strength; love and courage break him; he does not stalk you through your own dreams in this film; he is a flesh and blood thing in the room.
- Raw kills: on-screen 2 named (Schneider, Grady) + 6 party guests (fandom count; 6 or 7 per other sources) + Kerry (ambiguous) = 9. Implied 0.

### Dream Warriors (1987)
- Targets: Kristen Parker, Phillip Anderson, Jennifer Caulfield, Joey Crusel, Roland Kincaid, Taryn White, Will Stanton, Nancy Thompson, Don Thompson, Dr. Neil Gordon.
- Died: Phillip (dream, puppeted by his own veins off a ledge; real world fall). Jennifer (head into a TV). Taryn (dream heroin overdose). Will (stabbed). Don (real world, by Freddy's animated skeleton, impaled on a car fin). Nancy (stabbed by Freddy disguised as her father's ghost).
- Survived: Kristen (dream pulling power, then rescued by Nancy stabbing Freddy with his own glove). Joey (comatose, rescued by the group). Kincaid (super strength in dreams). Neil (incapacitated, then purified the bones with holy water).
- How hard: took a team with dream powers, group hypnosis, a nun's secret, and consecrating his bones. Everyone without a power or a rescue died.
- Share killed: 6 of 10 (60%).
- Weaknesses: hypnocil (experimental dream suppressant, shown in this film), pulling others into a shared dream, bones laid to rest with holy water, his own glove can hurt him.
- Raw kills: on-screen 6. Implied 0.

### The Dream Master (1988)
- Targets: Kincaid, Joey, Kristen, Sheila Kopecky, Rick Johnson, Debbie Stevens, Alice Johnson, Dan Jordan.
- Died: Kincaid (junkyard, stabbed, despite fighting well). Joey (pulled into waterbed). Kristen (thrown into the boiler; drugged asleep by her mother's sleeping pills). Sheila (life sucked out in a kiss, looks like asthma). Rick (killed by an invisible Freddy in a dojo, despite martial arts). Debbie (turned into a cockroach, crushed in a roach motel).
- Survived: Alice (absorbed Kristen's dream power and her dead friends' abilities, then showed him his reflection). Dan (wounded in the dream; surgeons woke him up in real life as he bled out, pure luck).
- How hard: the only win needed a girl carrying six dead friends' powers plus a rhyme about reflection. Even veterans with powers (Kincaid, Kristen, Joey) were killed in one night each.
- Share killed: 6 of 8 (75%). Of the three dream warriors he hunted, he killed all three.
- Weaknesses: his reflection (souls inside him revolt), someone waking you, Alice's absorbed powers.
- Raw kills: on-screen 6. Implied 0.

### The Dream Child (1989)
- Targets: Dan Jordan, Greta Gibson, Mark Gray, Yvonne Miller, Alice, the unborn Jacob.
- Died: Dan (fused with his motorcycle, crashed into a truck). Greta (force fed her own organs at a dinner party). Mark (turned to paper, shredded).
- Survived: Yvonne (rescued from a jacuzzi by Alice). Mark survived one attack because Alice saved him, then died the second time. Alice and Jacob, with help from Amanda Krueger's spirit.
- How hard: needed Alice's dream skills plus Amanda's soul plus Jacob turning Freddy's own power on him.
- Share killed: 3 of 6 (50%).
- Weaknesses: needs Alice's unborn child as a conduit (that conduit lets him reach her friends even while Alice is awake); Amanda's remains and spirit; Jacob rejecting him.
- Raw kills: on-screen 3. Implied 0.

### Freddy's Dead: The Final Nightmare (1991)
- Targets: per the opening, nearly every child and teenager in Springwood; on-screen John Doe, Carlos, Spencer, Tracy, Maggie (his daughter).
- Died: Carlos (dream, deafness exploited, head bursts). Spencer (video game dream, knocked into a pit). John Doe (dropped onto spikes; Freddy let him live earlier only to use him).
- Survived: Tracy (woken by Maggie). Maggie (pulled him into the real world and killed him with his glove, a knife, and a pipe bomb). Doc (has learned to control his dreams, UNCONFIRMED that he was ever directly attacked).
- How hard: it took his own daughter entering his mind with 3D glasses and fighting him with an armory, but once he was real, an ordinary adult beat him hand to hand.
- Share killed: 3 of 5 on-screen. Implied: almost all of Springwood's youth.
- Weaknesses: dream demons revive him only in the dream world; in the real world he can be permanently killed; toys with victims (game controller, hearing aid gag), which wastes time and gave Tracy time to be woken. Being bound to Springwood is UNCONFIRMED from my sources.
- Raw kills: on-screen 3. Implied "nearly every child and teenager" of a town (uncountable; treated as the highest implied total).

### Wes Craven's New Nightmare (1994)
- Targets: Chuck and Terry (effects crew), Chase Porter, Julie (babysitter), Heather Langenkamp, Dylan.
- Died: Chuck and Terry (killed by the living glove in the opening; listed by the fandom death list; the Wikipedia plot frames the opening as Heather's dream and does not mention their deaths, so the real world deaths are fandom sourced, second source UNCONFIRMED). Chase (falls asleep driving, slashed). Julie (killed in the hospital, dragged up the wall, while Dylan sleeps; Julie herself was awake and watching).
- Survived: Heather and Dylan, by following him into his boiler room and pushing him into his own furnace. Robert Englund disappears; fate not shown.
- How hard: Heather had to go into his world in the flesh; reality itself was rewriting around her.
- Share killed: 4 of 6 (67%).
- Weaknesses: fire (furnace); being fought in his own world. No drug, no ritual, no simple trick. It kills people who are awake through someone else's dream.
- Raw kills: on-screen 3 to 4 (Chase, Julie, plus Chuck and Terry from the opening). Implied 0.

### Freddy vs. Jason (2003)
- Targets: Gibb, Mark Davis, Lori Campbell, Will Rollins, Bill Freeburg (possessed), Jason himself.
- Died: Mark (dream, burned, "Freddy's Back" carved into him). Gibb was attacked by Freddy in a dream but Jason killed her in reality. Bill was possessed by Freddy, then cut in half by Jason. Every other body is Jason's.
- Survived: Lori (attacked more than once, pulled a fragment of him and then Freddy himself into the real world), Will, Jason. Jason punched through his torso; Lori beheaded him.
- How hard: he was beaten by teenagers plus a rival killer, but they also used Hypnocil and fire.
- Share killed personally: 1 of the 4 humans he struck at.
- Weaknesses: weakened because the town erased him and gave Hypnocil to anyone who dreamed of him; needs fear to regain power; pulled into reality he is outmatched; Jason stole his victims.
- Raw kills: on-screen 1 (Mark). Implied 0. Opening flashback child victims excluded (pre-film).

### A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010)
- Targets: the Badham Preschool class: Dean Russell, Kris Fowles, Jesse Braun, Nancy Holbrook, Quentin Smith, Marcus Yeon, plus the other kids from the class; Gwen Holbrook.
- Died: Dean (diner, dream throat cut, real world self cut). Kris (thrown around her room, slashed). Jesse (jail cell, impaled; Freddy toys with him about the brain living seven minutes, per Plugged In). Gwen (real world after the ending, blades through the head from a mirror). Marcus Yeon (fandom: head into a computer; actor Aaron Yoo listed in fandom cast; not in the Wikipedia plot, UNCONFIRMED). Other kids: Wikipedia says Nancy "discovers that all of them have been killed"; fandom names 8 (Lisa Harper, Bret Tanzer, Sukari McGill, Nancy Lumb, Elizabeth Cook, Marcus Yeon, Carrie Bush, Craig Jackson).
- Survived: Nancy and Quentin. Stolen adrenaline, Quentin waking Nancy with a shot to the heart, pulling Freddy out by his sweater, slitting his throat and burning the room. He still comes back and kills Gwen.
- How hard: sleep deprivation causes micro sleeps, so staying awake does not keep you out of his world. Only 2 of the whole class lived.
- Share killed: about 12 of 14 of the class plus Gwen.
- Weaknesses: can be pulled into reality, cut and burned there; adrenaline wakes you. But the "kill" did not hold.
- Raw kills: on-screen 4 (Dean, Kris, Jesse, Gwen). Implied 8 (the rest of the class, including Marcus, whose on-screen death is UNCONFIRMED). Total about 12.

### One line evidence per stat

Freddy vs. Jason. STR 5: in reality he trades blows with Jason but loses an arm and is beheaded by a teenager. INT 7: poses as Pamela Voorhees to send Jason to Elm Street, possesses Bill to dump the Hypnocil. KILL 1: Mark only. DREAM 4: weakened until fear returns, needed Jason to even get back in the door.

Freddy's Revenge. STR 7: physically in the real world, slaughters a pool party by hand, boils the pool. INT 3: one crude plan, ride Jesse's body, and no deceptions beyond that. KILL 6: about 9 bodies. DREAM 3: works through Jesse's nightmares, not yours; heat and fire tricks only.

The Dream Child. STR 4: almost no raw force shown; he wins by transformation, not muscle. INT 7: hides inside Alice, feeds souls to her unborn son to remake him in his image. KILL 3: Dan, Greta, Mark. DREAM 7: motorcycle fusion, force fed organs, comic book shredding.

1984. STR 6: survives fire and traps, drags Marge into the bed, overpowers Tina. INT 5: stages Rod's death as a suicide in his cell, but then walks straight into Nancy's traps. KILL 3: Tina, Rod, Glen. DREAM 8: the most frightening Freddy by fan consensus; drowning in a bathtub, phone tongue, the stretching arms.

Dream Warriors. STR 5: has to cheat with a skeleton to kill Don; Kincaid can grapple him in dreams. INT 8: disguises as Don's ghost to stab Nancy, picks each kid's weakness (Taryn's addiction, Jennifer's TV dream, Phillip's sleepwalking). KILL 5: 6 kills. DREAM 7: vein puppets, TV head, snake Freddy, but hypnocil and group dreaming can blunt him.

New Nightmare. STR 8: crosses into reality, kills an awake Julie, carries Dylan away. INT 7: weeks of phone calls, kills Heather's husband first to isolate her, wears her down. KILL 4: four victims. DREAM 8: the dream overrides reality, earthquakes, no drug works.

Freddy's Dead. STR 5: Maggie beats him hand to hand once he is real. INT 7: spares John Doe on purpose as bait to lead him to his daughter. KILL 10: nearly every child and teen in Springwood. DREAM 7: deadly but goofy (video game, hearing aid), fear diluted.

2010. STR 7: slams Kris around her room, throws Quentin into a mirror, keeps fighting after his throat is cut. INT 7: uses micro sleeps so you are never safe, toys with Jesse about seven minutes of brain life, comes back after an apparent death. KILL 8: about 12. DREAM 8: no jokes, sexual menace, micro sleep invasion while you think you are awake.

Dream Master. STR 8: beats Kincaid, who has dream strength, and Rick, who has dream martial arts. INT 8: tricks Kristen into summoning a fresh friend so he gets new victims; time loop trap for Alice and Dan. KILL 5: 6 kills. DREAM 10: cockroach transformation, invisible combat, life sucked out by a kiss, time loops; even powered dream warriors died in one night each.

### Reason for each percentage

- Freddy vs. Jason, 92%: he is the weakest he has ever been, he went after a whole group and personally killed one, Hypnocil exists, and when he is pulled into the real world even teenagers can finish him.
- Freddy's Revenge, 55%: slightly better than a coin flip. He cannot touch your dreams and is a flesh body you can run from, and most of a crowded party lived; but anyone left alone with him (Schneider, Grady) died.
- The Dream Child, 45%: a near coin flip. Exactly half the people he went after died, and the ones who lived had Alice dragging them out.
- 1984, 35%: worse than even. Three of his four teenage targets died, but the one who lived was an ordinary girl with coffee, traps, and the nerve to turn her back, a plan you could copy.
- Dream Warriors, 25%: one in four. Six of ten targets died, and the survivors needed dream powers, hypnocil and a ritual with his bones.
- New Nightmare, 20%: one in five. He kills people who are wide awake through someone else's dream, and the only way out was walking into his furnace room in the flesh.
- Freddy's Dead, 15%: he emptied a whole town of its children, and the only reason anyone survived is that he wasted time playing games.
- 2010, 10%: out of an entire preschool class only two lived, staying awake does not protect you because you fall into micro sleeps, and killing him did not stick.
- Dream Master, 5%: near certain death. He killed every dream warrior he hunted, powers and all, and the only ordinary survivor lived because surgeons happened to wake him.

## Fan consensus check

What fans say (Inside the Magic portrayal ranking; WebSearch summaries of multiple lists; Screen Rant film quality ranking; Reddit blocked):
- 1984 and Freddy's Revenge are the "darkest and most menacing" Freddy.
- Dream Warriors is the "perfect" or best balanced Freddy.
- New Nightmare's Freddy is "pretty terrifying."
- Dream Master, Dream Child and Freddy's Dead are lumped together as "goofy," Looney Tunes Freddy.
- Freddy vs. Jason's Freddy "isn't scary."
- 2010 is "equally vile" per some, dull per others; Screen Rant ranks the film last.

Agreement: Freddy vs. Jason is the safest here and fans also see him as the least scary. New Nightmare sits in the deadly half, matching fans. 2010 near the bottom matches the "vile" reading.

Disagreement and why:
- Dream Master as deadliest. Fans call him goofy, but this ranking measures results, and he has the highest kill rate against the best defended victims in the series. Jokes do not make the claws less sharp.
- Freddy's Dead in the deadly third. Fans rank it silliest, but the film states he wiped out a town's youth. Kill count is scored on facts, not tone; his tone is already reflected in a lower DREAM score.
- 1984 and Revenge in the safer half despite being the "scariest." Fear is not the same as lethality. In 1984 an ordinary person beat him with tools any viewer has; in Revenge he cannot reach your dreams at all.

## Least certain pairs

1. Freddy's Revenge (55) vs The Dream Child (45). For Revenge being safer: no dream access, physical body you can flee, most of the party lived. For Dream Child being safer: only 3 kills against Revenge's 9, and he needs Alice's pregnancy as a conduit. I kept Revenge safer because an ordinary person in Dream Child (Greta, Dan, Mark) died unless Alice intervened, while in Revenge the crowd could simply run.
2. The Dream Child (45) vs 1984 (35). For 1984 being safer: Nancy's counters (traps, waking, turning your back) need no powers. For Dream Child being safer: lower share of targets killed (50% vs 60 to 75%). I chose Dream Child safer on kill share; a case exists to swap them.
3. A Nightmare on Elm Street 2010 (10) vs The Dream Master (5). For 2010 being deadliest: about 12 kills, micro sleeps make staying awake useless, and he survives his "death." For Dream Master: he kills powered victims and has the widest dream toolkit, and his only ordinary survivor lived by medical luck. 2010's ordinary survivors (Nancy and Quentin) used ordinary tools (adrenaline), which is why I left 2010 one rung safer.
4. Freddy's Dead (15) vs New Nightmare (20). Freddy's Dead has the biggest implied body count, but on-screen he is beaten hand to hand by an adult and loses time clowning; New Nightmare's entity kills awake people and has no shown counter except his furnace. Swapping them is defensible.
5. Dream Warriors (25) vs New Nightmare (20). Dream Warriors kills more (6 vs 4) and has the best INT, but hypnocil exists and he can be ended with a ritual; New Nightmare has no drug and kills the awake. Close.
6. Freddy vs. Jason (92) vs everything else. The gap from 92 to 55 is large. It is justified because FvJ Freddy personally kills only one person, but if the owner wants a smoother ladder, FvJ could drop to about 85 or Revenge rise to about 65.
