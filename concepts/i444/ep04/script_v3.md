## s00 cold open

He will drag you across the ceiling, turn you into a puppet, smash your head through a TV, crush you like a bug, or pull you down into your own bed. And all he needs is for you to fall asleep. We're ranking every Freddy by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 rules

Here's the rule. You fall asleep tonight, and that movie's Freddy is waiting for you. And every Freddy is different. One is so weak he needs Jason to kill for him, one needs a teenager's body, and one is strong enough to wipe out kids with superpowers. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and dream power, which is how dangerous he gets once your eyes close. Put it all together, and you get one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #9 Freddy vs. Jason (2003), 95%, Easy

Number nine, Freddy versus Jason. This Freddy is the weakest of them all, because his town forgot him. The adults erased every trace of him, and anyone who still dreams about him gets locked away in a hospital and kept on a drug that stops them from dreaming. He's so weak that he has to bring Jason back from Hell to scare people for him. In a movie with his own name on the poster, Jason does nearly all the killing, and Freddy kills exactly one person himself, Mark. In the end, Lori cuts his head off with Jason's machete. If you've never heard his name, he can't even find you. Survival rate, ninety-five percent.

## s03 #8 Freddy's Revenge (1985), 65%, Don't Worry

Number eight, Freddy's Revenge. This Freddy is far more dangerous than the last one, because he doesn't need Jason. He takes over a teenager named Jesse, climbs right out of his body, and kills more people on screen than any other Freddy. He kills Jesse's gym teacher in the showers, then his friend Grady, then a whole group of kids at a pool party, and in the very last scene, his claws burst out of Lisa's friend Kerry on the school bus. But he's also the dumbest Freddy, he barely uses his dream powers, and he can be beaten by love. Lisa tells Jesse she loves him, kisses Freddy, and he burns to ash. Stay far away from Jesse, and you'll probably be fine. Survival rate, sixty-five percent.

## s04 #7 The Dream Child (1989), 55%, Close Call

Number seven, The Dream Child. This Freddy kills fewer people than the last one, but he's far smarter, and much stronger in your dreams. He finds a sneaky new way in, through the dreams of Alice's unborn son, Jacob, so he can come for her friends even while she's wide awake. He turns Dan into part of his own motorcycle, force-feeds Greta until she chokes, and turns Mark into a paper drawing before slicing him to pieces. But Alice has beaten him once already, and in the end, with help from Freddy's own mother, Jacob uses the power Freddy gave him to destroy him. He only gets three kills, so if he comes for you, it's close to a coin flip. Survival rate, fifty-five percent.

## s05 #6 Freddy's Dead (1991), 45%, Better Run

Number six, Freddy's Dead. Unlike the last one, this Freddy has the biggest body count of them all. Before the movie even starts, he has already killed almost every child and teenager in his town. But he's also the silliest Freddy, and he plays with his victims like toys. He turns up Carlos's hearing until one scratch on a chalkboard kills him, traps Spencer inside a video game, and drops John Doe, the last teenager from Springwood, onto a bed of spikes. All those jokes take time, and in that time, Tracy gets woken up before he can finish her. In the end, his own daughter, Maggie, drags him into the real world and shoves a pipe bomb into his chest. He's deadly, but he wastes time, and that time is your way out. Survival rate, forty-five percent.

## s06 #5 the 2010 remake, 38%, Almost Dead

Number five, the twenty ten remake. This Freddy is the opposite of the last one. He's dark, mean, and serious, and he has a trick that makes staying awake useless. Go too long without sleep, and your brain drops into his world for a few seconds at a time, without you even noticing. By the time Nancy figures out who he is, every other kid from their old preschool is dead except her and Quentin. He gets Dean in a diner, Kris in her bedroom, and Jesse in a jail cell, and even after Nancy drags him into the real world and cuts his throat, he comes back through a mirror for her mother. Staying awake won't save you from this one. Survival rate, thirty-eight percent.

## s07 #4 Wes Craven's New Nightmare (1994), 30%, Almost Dead

Number four, New Nightmare. This Freddy is stronger and smarter than the last one, and he doesn't just hunt teenagers, he hunts a whole family. He goes after Heather, the actress who played Nancy, and picks off the people around her one by one. He kills two special effects men working on a new Freddy movie, then her husband while he's driving, then her son's babysitter in a hospital, dragging her up the wall just like Tina. Then he takes her little boy, Dylan, right into his world. It takes Heather and Dylan together to push him into a furnace. He only gets four kills, but if he picks you, the people you love go first. Survival rate, thirty percent.

## s08 #3 A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984), 22%, Slaughtered

Number three, the original Nightmare on Elm Street, from nineteen eighty-four. This Freddy isn't as strong as the last one, but he's the scariest Freddy of them all. He barely talks, he stays in the shadows, and nobody believes you until it's too late. He drags Tina across the ceiling, strangles Rod with his own bedsheet in a jail cell, and pulls Glen down into his bed. Nancy only survives because she's smart enough to learn his rules herself. She sets traps, drags him into the real world, and finally turns her back on him, taking away the fear that feeds him, and even then, he still takes her mother. Unless you're as brave and as smart as Nancy, he gets you. Survival rate, twenty-two percent.

## s09 #2 Dream Warriors (1987), 12%, Slaughtered

Number two, Dream Warriors. This Freddy is deadlier than the last one, because kids who learn to fight back still can't stop him. He hunts the last children of Elm Street in a mental hospital. He turns Phillip into a puppet using his own veins, smashes Jennifer's head into a TV, and kills Taryn and Will even after they find their dream powers. Then his skeleton kills Nancy's father in the real world, and Freddy kills Nancy herself while pretending to be her dad, so even the girl who beat the last Freddy doesn't make it out. The kids who survived had superpowers, and it still took holy water on his bones to stop him. You don't have superpowers. Survival rate, twelve percent.

## s10 #1 The Dream Master (1988), 5%, Slaughtered

And number one, The Dream Master. This Freddy is the strongest of them all, the one who wiped out the Dream Warriors. Kincaid, Joey and Kristen, the kids who survived the last Freddy, are all dead early in the movie. Then he goes through Alice's friends one by one, Sheila, Rick, and Debbie, who he turns into a cockroach and crushes, and every soul he takes stays trapped inside him. Alice only wins by taking on her dead friends' powers and showing Freddy his own reflection, so the souls inside him break free and tear him apart. If the kids with superpowers couldn't survive this one, you won't either. Survival rate, five percent.
