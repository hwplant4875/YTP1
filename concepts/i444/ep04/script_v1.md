# EP04 script v1: Every Freddy Krueger Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving Him

Same build as EP03: s00 cold open over the fastest kill moments, s01 straight onto the stat card (no ground-rules lines), s02..s10 = #9 to #1, s11 outro.
Voice: Adam (s3TPKV1kjDlVtZbl4Ksh), eleven_v4. Each entry ends "Survival rate: N percent." and the % pops in tier colour with the tier cell.
Every kill claim checked against the films' Wikipedia plot sections (10/27).

## s00 cold open
He's been burned alive, buried in holy ground, blown up with a pipe bomb and dragged into the real world, and he still keeps coming back, because you can't run from a guy who waits for you to fall asleep. There are nine Freddys, and today we're ranking every single one of them.

## s01 scoring (stat card on screen from frame 1)
So every Freddy gets scored on four things: strength, intelligence, kill count, and dream power, which is basically how much he can do to you once you close your eyes. Put it all together and you get one number, your survival rate. And remember, you can't outrun this one, because sooner or later, everybody has to sleep.

## s02 #9 Freddy vs. Jason (2003), 80%, Don't Worry
Number nine is Freddy from Freddy vs. Jason. Everyone in Springwood has forgotten him, so he's too weak to hurt anybody, and he has to send Jason in to do the killing for him. In a movie with his own name on the poster, Freddy gets exactly one kill, and in the end Lori cuts his head off with Jason's own machete. Survival rate: eighty percent, unless your name is Mark.

## s03 #8 Freddy's Revenge (1985), 58%, Close Call
Number eight is Freddy's Revenge. This time he can't just get you in your dreams, he has to take over a teenager named Jesse and use his body. He still gets Jesse's gym coach, his best friend and a whole backyard pool party, but he's easy to see coming, and he gets beaten by a girl telling Jesse she loves him. Survival rate: fifty-eight percent.

## s04 #7 The Dream Child (1989), 50%, Better Run
Number seven is The Dream Child. Freddy gets back in through the dreams of Alice's unborn baby, which is a genuinely creepy trick, and he turns Dan into part of his motorcycle, makes Greta eat herself, and turns Mark into a comic book drawing. But Alice has beaten him before, and with help from Freddy's own mother, she does it again. Survival rate: fifty percent.

## s05 #6 A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010), 38%, Almost Dead
Number six is the 2010 remake. This Freddy is darker and way less funny, and he has a nasty new trick, micro-naps, so if you've been awake too long, you can drop into his world for a few seconds without even noticing. He gets Dean, Kris and Jesse, and even after Nancy slits his throat, he comes back for her mother in the very last scene. Survival rate: thirty-eight percent.

## s06 #5 Freddy's Dead: The Final Nightmare (1991), 30%, Almost Dead
Number five is Freddy's Dead. On screen he's at his most cartoonish, with a video game kill and a hearing aid gag, but don't let that fool you. By the time this movie starts, he's already killed almost every kid in Springwood, and the adults who are left have completely lost it. It took his own daughter dragging him into the real world and shoving a pipe bomb into his chest to end it. Survival rate: thirty percent.

## s07 #4 A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984), 25%, Slaughtered
Number four is the original. This is Freddy at his scariest, barely talking and always in the shadows. He drags Tina across the ceiling, makes Rod's death look like a suicide and pulls Glen into his own bed, and nobody believes Nancy until it's too late. She survives by setting traps, dragging him into the real world and finally turning her back on him, and even then, the last scene says he never really left. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s08 #3 Dream Warriors (1987), 20%, Slaughtered
Number three is Dream Warriors. Freddy goes after the last kids of Elm Street, locked in a psychiatric hospital, and he gets creative. He turns Phillip into a puppet, puts Jennifer's head through a TV, and kills Nancy herself while pretending to be her father. The kids only make it because they learn to fight back with dream powers and bury his bones in holy ground. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s09 #2 The Dream Master (1988), 12%, Slaughtered
Number two is The Dream Master. Freddy comes back and wipes out the last Dream Warriors, Kristen, Kincaid and Joey, the kids who already knew how to fight him. Then he goes through Alice's friends one by one, and every soul he takes makes him stronger. Alice only wins by taking her dead friends' powers and showing Freddy his own reflection. Survival rate: twelve percent.

## s10 #1 New Nightmare (1994), 8%, Slaughtered
And number one is New Nightmare. This isn't the Freddy from the movies anymore. It's something ancient that liked being Freddy so much that it broke out of the films and into the real world. It goes after Heather Langenkamp, the actress who played Nancy, kills her husband and her son's babysitter, and drags her little boy into its world. To stop it, she has to step into the story and become Nancy one last time. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s11 outro
So that's every Freddy, from the one who needed Jason's help to the one who broke into the real world. Tell me in the comments which Freddy you'd put at number one, and if you want Pinhead next, you know where to ask.
