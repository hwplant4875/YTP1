## s00 cold open
A clown can wrap you in a cotton candy cocoon, drag you under your bed, hunt you through a cornfield, pull you down a storm drain, and saw you in half, and the scariest one of all never says a single word. We're ranking ten horror movie clowns by your chance of surviving one that comes for you tonight.

## s01 scoring
And this time the question is simple: if this clown showed up at your house tonight, what are your odds? Every clown gets scored on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how supernatural it is, which really means whether fighting back even works, and that gives you one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #10 Clown zombie, Zombieland (2009), 99%, Don't Worry
Number ten is the clown zombie from Zombieland. He's terrifying for about three seconds, until you notice he's slow, he can't open a door, and he's wearing a wig. Columbus is scared of clowns more than anything in the world, and even he takes this one out with a carnival mallet. Survival rate: ninety-nine percent.

## s03 #9 Clown doll, Poltergeist (2015), 95%, Don't Worry
Number nine is the clown doll from Poltergeist. It drags Griffin under the bed and tries to strangle him, and the whole house is clearly on its side, but here's the thing, nobody in this family actually dies. Pull the doll off, run out of the house, and don't let your little sister talk to the TV. Survival rate: ninety-five percent.

## s04 #8 Captain Spaulding, House of 1000 Corpses (2003), 60%, Close Call
Number eight is Captain Spaulding. He runs a gas station with a murder ride in the back, and when two robbers try to hold him up, he shoots them both without losing his smile. He's just an old man with a gun, so you can outrun him, but if he sends you down the road to see Dr. Satan, his family is waiting at the end of it. Survival rate: sixty percent.

## s05 #7 Killer Klowns from Outer Space (1988), 45%, Better Run
Number seven is Killer Klowns from Outer Space. They land in a circus tent spaceship, wrap people in cotton candy cocoons, and drink them through a straw, and there are dozens of them. But they have one huge weakness, their noses. One shot to the nose and they pop like a balloon, so if you know the trick, you have a real chance. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s06 #6 Frendo, Clown in a Cornfield (2025), 40%, Better Run
Number six is Frendo from Clown in a Cornfield. Frendo isn't one killer, it's the whole town, the mayor, the sheriff, even a teacher, all wearing the same smiling mask, and they hunt the teenagers through the corn with chainsaws, crossbows, and pitchforks. They're only human, so you can fight back, but when the police are the ones in the masks, there's nobody left to call. Survival rate: forty percent.

## s07 #5 Pennywise, It (1990), 30%, Almost Dead
Number five is the original Pennywise, from the 1990 miniseries. He comes back to Derry every twenty-seven years, takes Georgie through a storm drain, and can turn into whatever you're most afraid of. He's beaten twice, but only when seven kids stand together, and as an adult he still takes Eddie down with him. Survival rate: thirty percent.

## s08 #4 Pennywise, It (2017), 25%, Almost Dead
Number four is Pennywise from It in 2017. In Derry, kids go missing at six times the national average, and he can show up anywhere, in the sewer, the library basement, or a slideshow in your garage. He takes Georgie, Betty Ripsom and Patrick Hockstetter, and the Losers only beat him by refusing to be afraid. Alone at night, that's a lot harder than it sounds. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s09 #3 Pennywise, It Chapter Two (2019), 15%, Slaughtered
Number three is Pennywise in It Chapter Two, and this time he isn't only hunting kids. He kills Adrian under a bridge, lures a little girl under the bleachers, and stabs Eddie through the chest. It takes six grown adults, an ancient ritual, and shouting at a giant spider until he shrinks before he finally dies. You won't have six friends with you. Survival rate: fifteen percent.

## s10 #2 Art the Clown, Terrifier (2016), 6%, Slaughtered
Number two is Art the Clown in the first Terrifier. He doesn't talk, he doesn't run, and he doesn't need a reason, he just keeps coming, and he goes through almost everyone he meets in one night. The only survivor is found alive but so badly hurt she's never the same again. Survival rate: six percent.

## s11 #1 Art the Clown, Terrifier 2 (2022), 3%, Slaughtered
And number one is Art the Clown in Terrifier 2. He comes back from the dead, kills the coroner, and spends Halloween night hunting Sienna and her little brother, and fighting back barely matters, because death doesn't stop him. Even Sienna has to die and come back before she can cut his head off, and he still isn't done. Survival rate: three percent.

## s12 outro
So that's ten clowns, from a zombie in a wig to one you can't even kill. Tell me in the comments which clown you think you'd survive if it showed up at your door tonight, and maybe don't go to the circus for a while.
