## 한눈에 보기
- 형식은 EP01 마이어스 편과 같아요: 쉬운 놈부터 불가능한 놈까지 카운트다운, 실제 영화 클립 위주, 항목마다 SURVIVAL RATE(90%에서 시작해 1%까지 들쭉날쭉하게 하락) + 짧은 INSTRUCTION, 오른쪽 위 작은 영화 제목, Adam 목소리.
- 영화 11편 = 제이슨 11명. 1편(1980)은 범인이 엄마라서 인트로에서 반전으로만 쓰고, 5편(1985)은 가짜 제이슨이라 중간에 짧은 보너스로만 넣어요.
- 길이 목표: 약 8분.
- 제목 후보 (썸네일과 짝):
  1. Every Jason Voorhees Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving Him (추천, 3분할 썸네일)
  2. Every Jason Voorhees Ranked by How Hard He Is to Kill
  3. Only 1% Survive This Jason Voorhees
- 썸네일: 처음부터 공식 고화질 스틸로. 3분할 90% → 50% → 1% (Part 2 자루 제이슨 → Jason Lives 좀비 → Jason X 우버 제이슨).

## SCRIPT (English narration)

**[COLD OPEN]**
He has been hacked, hanged, drowned, electrocuted, blown to pieces, frozen and thrown into space.
And he kept coming back.
Here is the twist: in the first movie, the killer was not even him. It was his mother.
Every movie after that gave us a different Jason. Eleven of them.
Today we rank every one, from the one you could beat... to the one you should never meet.
This is Instruction four four four.

**[RULES]**
Same rule as always. Not the best movie. The hardest Jason to kill. And for every Jason, one number: your survival rate. It starts high. It will not stay high.

**[#11 PART 2, 1981]**
Number eleven. Friday the Thirteenth Part Two.
No hockey mask yet. Just a grown man with a sack on his head, living in a shack in the woods.
He is strong, but he is human. Ginny puts on his mother's sweater, talks to him like his mom, and drives a machete into his shoulder.
Survival rate: ninety percent. Instruction: pretend to be his mom.

**[#10 FRIDAY THE 13TH, 2009]**
Number ten. The remake.
This Jason runs. He sets traps. He keeps a prisoner in his tunnels.
But he is still flesh and blood. In the end he is pulled into a wood chipper, hit with a machete, and dumped in the lake.
Survival rate: eighty-two percent. Instruction: don't hide in the tunnels.

**[#9 PART III, 1982]**
Number nine. Part Three. The movie where he finds the hockey mask.
Chris hangs him from the barn. He pulls himself free. Then she buries an axe in his head.
He gets up. Again.
Survival rate: seventy-one percent. Instruction: aim for the head. Twice.

**[#8 THE FINAL CHAPTER, 1984]**
Number eight. The Final Chapter.
The morgue thinks he is dead. He is not. He walks out and kills his way to a house in the woods.
And the one who stops him is a twelve-year-old boy. Tommy shaves his head to look like young Jason, distracts him, and brings a machete down on his face.
Survival rate: sixty percent. Instruction: shave your head. Trust me.

**[BONUS: A NEW BEGINNING]**
Quick note on Part Five. The killer in a hockey mask is not Jason. It is a paramedic named Roy. Fake Jason. He does not count.

**[#7 JASON TAKES MANHATTAN, 1989]**
Number seven. Jason Takes Manhattan.
He rides a cruise ship to New York, survives the ocean, and gets punched by a boxer on a rooftop.
But the sewers of Manhattan flood with toxic waste every night. It melts him down until he turns back into a little boy.
Survival rate: forty-seven percent. Instruction: find the sewer.

**[#6 THE NEW BLOOD, 1988]**
Number six. The New Blood.
Tina has telekinesis. She electrocutes him, hangs him with a power cable, sets him on fire and drops a house on him.
He survives all of it. It takes her dead father, rising out of the lake, to drag him under.
Survival rate: thirty-eight percent. Instruction: don't fight alone.

**[MID-ROLL]**
Still alive at number six? Subscribe to survive the rest.

**[#5 JASON GOES TO HELL, 1993]**
Number five. Jason Goes to Hell.
In the first five minutes, the FBI blows him to pieces. Story over? No.
His heart keeps beating. Whoever touches it becomes Jason. He jumps from body to body until hands from hell drag him down.
Survival rate: twenty-six percent. Instruction: don't touch the heart.

**[#4 FREDDY VS. JASON, 2003]**
Number four. Freddy versus Jason.
He takes on a dream demon. He is stabbed, set on fire and slashed again and again. Freddy throws everything at him.
In the end, Jason walks out of Crystal Lake holding Freddy's head.
Survival rate: fifteen percent. Instruction: let them fight.

**[#3 JASON LIVES, 1986]**
Number three. Jason Lives.
He was dead and buried. Then lightning hits a metal post in his chest, and he wakes up as something else. Bullets do nothing.
No one kills him. Tommy chains him to a boulder at the bottom of the lake, and he is still down there, waiting.
Survival rate: seven percent. Instruction: chain him. Don't kill him.

**[#2 JASON X, 2001]**
Number two. Jason X.
The government cannot kill him, so they freeze him. Four hundred years later, he wakes up on a spaceship.
They freeze his head in liquid nitrogen and smash it to pieces.
Survival rate: three percent. Instruction: stay frozen.

**[#1 UBER JASON]**
Number one. Same movie, but not the same Jason.
Medical nanobots rebuild him with a body of metal. Uber Jason.
He is finally blasted into the atmosphere and burns up over the planet. And his mask still lands in a lake.
This is not a man. This is Friday the thirteenth.
Survival rate: one percent. Instruction: leave the planet.

**[OUTRO]**
Eleven Jasons. One lake he always comes back to.
Tell us your ranking in the comments. Which Jason could you actually survive?
This was Instruction four four four.
