Why each sits where it does:
- **Annabelle Comes Home 95:** nobody dies. She wakes every spirit in the Warrens' room (the Ferryman, the Bride, the Black Shuck hellhound, the Feeley Meeley hands), but two teenage girls and a ten-year-old end it in one night by locking the doll back in its case.
- **Annabelle 80:** it never kills anyone with its own hands. It needs a soul to be offered, so it sets a fire, attacks Father Perez and takes the baby to force a trade. The only death is Evelyn, who gives her own life in Mia's place.
- **The Conjuring 65:** the only thing Bathsheba kills on screen is the family dog, but she possesses Carolyn completely and nearly makes her kill her own daughters. Ed, who isn't even a priest, sends her back with an exorcism.
- **The Conjuring 2 55:** kills nobody in Enfield, but it hides behind Bill Wilkins' ghost, blocks Lorraine's visions, attacks Billy as the Crooked Man, possesses Janet and nearly kills her and Ed. Saying its name beats it.
- **Annabelle: Creation 45:** four deaths: Samuel, Esther (ripped in half), and twelve years later the Higginses. It throws Janice from the stairlift and possesses her for good, yet six girls and Sister Charlotte still walk out.
- **The Devil Made Me Do It 30:** her curse makes you the killer. Arne stabs his landlord 22 times, Jessica stabs Katie and then dies in a fall, the Occultist cuts her own father's throat, gives Ed a heart attack and nearly makes him kill Lorraine. Four dead, and only destroying her altar stops it.
- **Last Rites 20:** the demon that killed Judy Warren the day she was born. It controls three ghosts, kills Father Gordon in a single visit, possesses Judy and nearly kills her a second time, and stops Ed's heart. Only the Warrens praying together destroy it.
- **The Nun II 12:** lifts a priest into the air and burns him alive, snaps a delivery girl's neck, kills the headmistress disguised as her dead son, and leaves a trail of deaths across Europe while living inside Maurice. Irene only survives the fire because she descends from St. Lucy.
- **The Nun 5:** at its own rift, Valak killed every nun in the abbey; the sisters Irene prays with were already dead. It buries Father Burke alive, possesses and drowns Irene (she is brought back), and escapes inside Maurice.

## Every death per film (checked on the Wikipedia plots, the Conjuring fandom wiki and the horror fandom wiki, 11/7)

Lines marked "never shown" are deaths we name in the narration but never put on screen (no suicide act, owner rule).

- **Annabelle Comes Home (2019, set 1972):** no deaths. Daniela opens the case; the Bride possesses Daniela, the Black Shuck attacks Bob, the Ferryman chases Mary Ellen, the demon attacks Judy, a phone voice says to offer Annabelle a soul, telekinesis holds the case door open. Judy, Mary Ellen and Daniela lock her back in.
- **Annabelle (2014, set 1967):**
  - Pete and Sharon Higgins: murdered by their cult daughter Annabelle and her boyfriend in a home invasion, before the demon's haunting starts.
  - The boyfriend: shot by the police.
  - Annabelle Higgins: dies holding the doll (never shown).
  - Evelyn: gives her life in Mia's place, the soul the demon wanted (never shown).
  - The demon itself: attacks Father Perez (hospitalised), sets the fire, appears as the horned demon, uses Father Perez's shape to take baby Leah.
- **The Conjuring (2013, set 1971):**
  - Sadie the dog: found dead in the yard.
  - Backstory only, not counted: Bathsheba sacrificed her baby in 1863 and cursed the land, and Lorraine sees a mother Bathsheba once possessed and made kill her child.
  - In the film: bruises, clocks stopping at 3:07, night attacks on the girls, an attack on Judy at the Warrens' home, then full possession of Carolyn, who takes Christine and April back to the house to kill them. Ed exorcises her and Lorraine reaches Carolyn with a family memory. All seven Perrons survive.
- **The Conjuring 2 (2016, set 1976 and 1977):**
  - No deaths in Enfield.
  - In the Amityville seance Lorraine relives the DeFeo murders and meets Valak. The film does not say Valak caused them, so they are not counted.
  - Valak uses Bill Wilkins' ghost to possess Janet, appears as the Crooked Man (Billy), shows Lorraine Ed's death, and nearly takes Janet and Ed out of the window by the impaling stump. Lorraine names it Valak and condemns it.
- **Annabelle: Creation (2017, set 1955, epilogue 12 years later):**
  - Samuel Mullins: possessed Janice breaks his fingers and kills him.
  - Esther Mullins: ripped in half, off screen. Years earlier the demon had gouged out her eye.
  - Pete and Sharon Higgins: murdered twelve years later by the grown, still possessed Janice, now called Annabelle.
  - It hurls Janice from the stairlift, possesses her, nearly drags Linda into the well and attacks Sister Charlotte. Sister Charlotte, Linda, Carol, Nancy, Kate and Tierney survive.
- **The Devil Made Me Do It (2021, set 1981):**
  - Bruno Sauls: stabbed 22 times by the cursed Arne.
  - Katie Lincoln: stabbed 22 times by the cursed Jessica.
  - Jessica: dies in a fall while cursed (never shown).
  - Father Kastner: the Occultist, his daughter Isla, slits his throat.
  - Isla herself: killed by the demon she summoned, when her curse fails.
  - David's possession, Ed's heart attack; the curse nearly makes Ed stab Lorraine and Arne kill himself. Ed destroys the altar.
- **The Conjuring: Last Rites (2025, set 1964 and 1986):**
  - Father Gordon: attacked and possessed at the Diocese of Scranton, dies (never shown).
  - Baby Judy: stillborn in 1964, revived by Lorraine's prayers.
  - Judy again in 1986: possessed and nearly killed; Ed saves her and Lorraine's prayer revives her (never shown).
  - The demon controls three spirits (an axe man who killed his wife and her mother, in the past), drops the kitchen light, knocks Judy out, gives Ed a cardiac episode, attacks Lorraine in the basement and survives Ed's exorcism. The Warrens' prayer destroys the mirror. The Smurls survive.
- **The Nun II (2023, set 1956):**
  - Father Noiret: lifted into the air and burned alive.
  - A delivery girl: neck snapped.
  - Madame Laurent, the headmistress: killed by Valak disguised as her dead son Cedric.
  - Also the series of deaths across Europe that makes the Church send Irene.
  - Maurice has been possessed since 1952; the demon goat from the stained glass attacks the students; Irene is lifted and set ablaze but does not burn. The wine turned into the blood of Christ banishes Valak and frees Maurice.
- **The Nun (2018, set 1952):**
  - The older nun in the opening, killed behind the door.
  - Sister Victoria: dies so Valak can't use her (never shown).
  - Every other nun of Saint Cartha's: the nuns Irene meets turn out to be dead, and Victoria was the last.
  - Frenchie is attacked by Victoria's corpse, Father Burke is buried alive, and Irene is possessed and drowned (Frenchie revives her). Irene spits the Blood of Christ into Valak's face and the rift is sealed, but Valak leaves inside Maurice.
