## 1. Film-by-film facts

### 1.1 Annabelle Comes Home (2019), currently #9, 95%

(a) Entity. The Annabelle doll is a "beacon for other spirits" [TMS]. It is a conduit for a horned demon that wants a soul [TMS][WP]. Once it is out of its blessed case it wakes the other artifacts in the Warrens' room (the Ferryman, the Bride, the samurai armor, the Feeley Meeley game, the Black Shuck hellhound).

(b) Deaths and attacks, in order.
1. Before the film: Daniela's father died in a car accident with Daniela driving. She is not at fault [WP][TMS].
2. Opening drive (1968), real: the road is closed for a crash, and Lorraine sees the ghost of a young woman from the wreck (she died before the scene). At the cemetery, spirits drawn by the doll push Ed into the road and a truck nearly hits him. The driver says the truck "lost control" [TMS]. This is the doll's work while it is OUT of the case.
3. Daniela opens the case to set the tipped-over doll upright, leaves it unlocked, and later the doll falls out [TMS]. Afterwards the doll moves on its own: the rocking chair, then Judy's bed.
4. The Bride stalks Judy and moves at her with a blade. [TMS] says dagger and [PI] says cleaver. Judy holds up a cross [TMS].
5. The Black Shuck hunts Bob outside. He hides in the chicken coop and a chicken is eaten. That chicken is the only on-screen death in the film [TMS].
6. Daniela is trapped in the artifact room. Her father's mutilated ghost blames her. The TV shows her own future, bloody [TMS][PI]. A spirit attack makes Daniela believe she has been stabbed in the belly, but it is an illusion [PI].
7. The Ferryman stalks Mary Ellen (coins) and tries to pull her to him. Her flashlight drives him off [TMS].
8. Judy in bed. Something pulls her leg, the doll is under the sheets, and the ghost of Annabelle Higgins screams at her. A silhouette show reveals the horned demon [TMS].
9. A phone call in "Lorraine's" voice turns sinister and says the doll wants a soul [TMS][WP].
10. The Black Shuck chases Judy at the car. Bob smashes his guitar into its face [TMS].
11. The samurai armor nearly crushes Mary Ellen [TMS].
12. Feeley Meeley demon hands grab at them [TMS][WP].
13. The Bride possesses Daniela, who attacks Mary Ellen. Judy plays Ed's exorcism film onto her [TMS][WP].
14. The horned demon attacks Judy and tries to take her soul. Judy repels it with a cross [TMS].
15. The demon holds the case door open by force. Daniela arrives, helps shut it, and has the key to lock it [WP].
Deaths of people in the film: none (2 src: [WP][TMS]; [PI] lists no death). The only death on screen is the chicken.

(c) In your room. The doll moves by itself, appears in your bed, pulls at your legs, fakes a parent's voice on the phone, and turns every haunted object in the house loose. Leaving: Bob is attacked outside the house, and on the road in the opening it reaches Ed outside the case too. It does not follow anyone home after it is re-locked.

(d) How the survivors got away. Two teenagers and a ten-ish girl (age not stated), with no adult and no priest, found the doll, carried it back, forced the door shut and locked it. An ordinary person alone could do the same. The only tools were a cross, a flashlight and a key. Nobody was seriously hurt.

(e) Weaknesses. The blessed case and its lock, crosses, light (the Ferryman), and Ed's exorcism film.

(c) In your room. It follows the family when they move house [WP]. It starts fires, moves objects, claws you and throws a grown man across a churchyard. It steals your baby from the crib. It never kills by its own hand. It pressures you to kill yourself.

(d) How the survivors got away. Mia and John were not saved by any skill of their own. Someone else (Evelyn) gave her life. Mia survived several attacks alone, which shows an ordinary person can live through it moment to moment. To end it, someone had to die.

(e) Weaknesses. It needs an offered soul. Church holy ground slowed it only a little (Perez was attacked at the church doors).

(c) In your room. Bruises while you sleep, your feet and hair pulled in bed, a figure dropping on you from the wardrobe, being locked in the cellar, someone you love turned into your killer. Leaving does NOT help. Lorraine says the entity has latched on to the family "so that even leaving the house will not free them" [WP], and it drags Carolyn and two girls back from the motel [WP]. It also reaches out to the Warrens' own house (Judy). This is the strongest "you cannot run" evidence in the first six films.

(d) How the survivors got away. Two professional demonologists, a police officer, an assistant and the husband restrained Carolyn, and Ed performed an exorcism without Church approval [WP]. An ordinary person alone could not do this. It needed an exorcism and the bond of a loving family member.

(e) Weaknesses. The exorcism rite, a mother's love (the beach memory), daylight (relatively). Crucially, her kills in her own film are all past ones. In 1971 she kills only a dog.

### Flag 1. The Nun II delivery victim (seized and snapped neck)
