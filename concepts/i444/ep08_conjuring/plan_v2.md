## Changes from v1

The three spirits' murders (a husband who killed his wife and her mother) happened in the past. Judy is nearly killed twice (stillborn in 1964 and revived; possessed in 1986), Ed has a cardiac episode, Ed's exorcism fails, and the Warrens' prayer together ("perfect love casts out fear") breaks the mirror. Strong, but far fewer deaths than Creation or the curse film.

- **Devil Made Me Do It: #4 -> #3**, odds unchanged (30%).
- **The Nun II:** the delivery victim is "a young girl" in most reviews (one synopsis says a boy): confirm on the clip before voicing. "Killed disguised as her dead son Cedric" (v1) is unconfirmed, so the script only says "kills the headmistress". Valak hunts the Eyes of Saint Lucy to regain the power it had as an angel (Wikipedia + Slashfilm). Maurice possessed since 1952 (4 years).
- **The Nun:** the nuns Irene prays with were not real; Victoria was the last nun and the Abbess was dead all along (Wikipedia, Looper).
