## s00 cold open

These demons will burn you alive in midair, bury you alive, snap your neck, make you stab your best friend twenty-two times, or turn your own mother against you. And sometimes, all it takes is a doll. We're ranking every Conjuring demon by your chance of surviving it.

## s01 rules

Here's the rule. Tonight, the demon from one of these movies comes for you. And every one is different. One can't touch you unless someone opens a glass case, one needs you to hand over your soul, and one has wiped out an entire abbey of nuns. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and possession, which is how completely it can take over your body. Put it all together, and you get one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #9 Annabelle Comes Home (2019), 95%, Easy

Number nine, Annabelle Comes Home. This Annabelle is the weakest demon of them all, because it's locked inside a blessed glass case in the Warrens' room of cursed objects, and as long as that glass stays shut, it can't touch anyone. When a teenager sneaks in and lets the doll out, it wakes up everything in that room. The Ferryman chases the babysitter, the Bride takes over her friend, a hellhound attacks a boy outside, and a board game grabs at them with dead hands. But in the whole movie, nobody dies, and it's beaten by two teenagers and a ten-year-old girl who simply put the doll back in its case and lock it. Leave the glass shut, and you'll be fine. Survival rate, ninety-five percent.

## s03 #8 Annabelle (2014), 80%, Don't Worry

Number eight, Annabelle. This demon is more dangerous than the last one, because it isn't locked away, it lives in the doll inside your own home. But it has one big weakness. It can't just take your life, it needs a soul that's offered to it. So it plays with you first, starting a fire in the kitchen and chasing you through the basement. Then it steals Mia's baby to force a trade, the baby for her soul. In the end, the only person who dies is Evelyn, who gives up her own life in Mia's place. Never offer it your soul, and it can't take you. Survival rate, eighty percent.

## s04 #7 The Conjuring (2013), 65%, Don't Worry

Number seven, The Conjuring. This demon is far more dangerous than Annabelle, because it doesn't need a doll or a deal. Bathsheba was a witch who gave her own baby to the devil, and she curses anyone who lives on her land. She stops every clock in the house at three oh seven, leaves bruises on the mother while she sleeps, pulls at the girls in their beds, and kills the family dog. Then she takes over the mother, Carolyn, completely, because in every family she gets inside, she makes the mother kill her own children. The Perrons only survive because Ed Warren does the exorcism himself, and Lorraine reaches Carolyn with a happy memory. She only wants the mother, so if that's not you, you'll probably live. Survival rate, sixty-five percent.

## s05 #6 The Conjuring 2 (2016), 55%, Close Call

Number six, The Conjuring Two. This is the first time we meet Valak, and it's much smarter than the last demon, even though it doesn't kill anyone. It hides behind the ghost of an old man, Bill Wilkins, so everyone thinks it's just a ghost. It blocks Lorraine's visions so she can't see the truth, and sends the Crooked Man after the youngest boy. Then it takes over Janet, and Ed has to fight his way in to save her. But Valak has one fatal weakness. If you know its name, you have power over it, and Lorraine sends it back to Hell just by saying it. Learn the name, and you can win. Survival rate, fifty-five percent.

## s06 #5 The Conjuring: Last Rites (2025), 45%, Better Run

Number five, Last Rites. This demon is stronger and smarter than that Valak, and it came closer than any other to killing the Warrens themselves. It lives inside an old mirror, and in nineteen sixty-four, it nearly killed Judy Warren on the day she was born. Twenty-two years later, it comes back for her. It uses three ghosts to haunt the Smurl family, drives the priest who tries to bless their house to his death, gives Ed a heart attack, and takes over Judy. Ed's exorcism doesn't even work, and only the whole family together, Ed, Lorraine and Judy, can break the mirror. If it wants you, your family is the only thing standing in its way. Survival rate, forty-five percent.

## s07 #4 Annabelle: Creation (2017), 40%, Almost Dead

Number four, Annabelle Creation. This is where the Annabelle demon began, and unlike the last one, it kills almost everyone in its house. It pretends to be Bee, the dead daughter of a doll maker, so her heartbroken parents let it into the doll. Then it goes after the orphan girls who move in. It drags Janice out of her wheelchair into a barn and takes over her body for good. Through her, it breaks the doll maker's fingers and kills him, and twelve years later, it kills the couple who adopted her. Five girls and a nun make it out, but only because they run, and the demon stays inside Janice. If you're the weakest one in the house, it picks you. Survival rate, forty percent.

## s08 #3 The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It (2021), 30%, Almost Dead

Number three, The Devil Made Me Do It. This one is the smartest of them all, because the demon never needs to touch you. A witch puts a curse on you, and the demon makes you the killer. Arne Johnson stabs his landlord twenty-two times. A girl named Jessica stabs her best friend Katie twenty-two times, and then dies too. The witch kills her own father, and turns Ed against Lorraine until he nearly kills her himself. You can't run from a curse, because the one attacking is you, and only smashing the witch's altar can break it. Survival rate, thirty percent.

## s09 #2 The Nun II (2023), 12%, Slaughtered

Number two, The Nun Two. This is Valak again, and this Valak is far stronger than any demon so far. It's hunting for the eyes of Saint Lucy, a holy relic that would give it back the power it had as an angel, and it has been hiding inside Frenchie, the man who saved Irene, for four years. It lifts a priest into the air and burns him alive, snaps a young girl's neck, kills the headmistress of a boarding school, and leaves a trail of deaths across Europe. Sister Irene only survives being set on fire because she comes from Saint Lucy's own family, and it takes a miracle, wine turning into the blood of Christ, to drive it out. You're not from Saint Lucy's family. Survival rate, twelve percent.

## s10 #1 The Nun (2018), 5%, Slaughtered

And number one, The Nun. This is Valak at full power, guarding its own doorway out of Hell in an abbey in Romania. Before Irene even arrives, it has killed every nun in the abbey, and the sisters she prays with turn out to have been dead the whole time. It buries Father Burke alive in a coffin and drowns Irene in a flooded crypt. The Church sent a priest, a nun, and the actual blood of Christ, and even then, Valak gets away, hiding inside Frenchie. If the Vatican's best can only send it away, you have no chance. Survival rate, five percent.
