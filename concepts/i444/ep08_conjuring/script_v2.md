Title: Every Conjuring Demon Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving It

## s00 cold open

These demons will burn you alive in midair, bury you alive, snap your neck, make you stab your best friend twenty-two times, or turn your own mother against you. And sometimes, all it takes is a doll. We're ranking every Conjuring demon by your chance of surviving it.

## s01 rules

Here's the rule. Tonight, the demon from one of these movies is in your room, and it wants you. And every one is different. One is a doll that wakes up every cursed thing in the house, one wants you to give up your own life, and one wiped out an entire abbey of nuns. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and possession, which is how completely it can take over your body. Put it all together, and you get one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #9 Annabelle Comes Home (2019), 95%, Easy

Number nine, Annabelle Comes Home. This Annabelle is the weakest demon of them all, because even when it gets loose and wakes up every cursed object in the Warrens' house, it doesn't manage to kill a single person. The Ferryman chases the babysitter, the Bride takes over her friend, a hellhound hunts a boy in the yard, and the demon behind the doll turns up in a little girl's bed, and later tries to take her soul. In the end, two teenagers and a little girl beat it on their own, by forcing the doll back into its case and locking it. Even loose in your room all night, it will scare you half to death, but you'll almost certainly see morning. Survival rate, ninety-five percent.

## s03 #8 Annabelle (2014), 80%, Don't Worry

Number eight, Annabelle. This demon is more dangerous than the last one, because it doesn't just scare you, it hurts you, inside your own home. It sets your kitchen on fire, throws books at your baby, and hurls a priest through the air at the door of his own church. But it has one big weakness. It can't just take your life, it needs a soul that's given to it, so it steals Mia's baby to force a trade, the baby for her soul. Five people die in this movie, but the demon only gets the soul it came for when Evelyn gives up her own life in Mia's place. It will burn your home and throw you around, but as long as you never give it your soul, you'll probably live. Survival rate, eighty percent.

## s04 #7 The Conjuring (2013), 65%, Don't Worry

Number seven, The Conjuring. This demon is far more dangerous than Annabelle, because it doesn't need a doll or a deal, it gets inside your body, and moving out won't save you. Bathsheba was a witch who gave her own baby to the devil, and she curses anyone who lives on her land. She stops every clock in the house at three oh seven, leaves bruises on you while you sleep, drags the girls by their hair and their feet, and on the very first morning, the family dog is found dead in the yard. Then she takes over the mother, Carolyn, completely, and makes her try to kill her own children. The Perrons only survive because Lorraine reaches Carolyn with a happy memory, and Ed does the exorcism himself. She will follow you, hurt you, and climb inside you, but in her own movie she never manages to kill a single person. Survival rate, sixty-five percent.

## s05 #6 The Conjuring 2 (2016), 55%, Close Call

Number six, The Conjuring Two. This is the movie that introduced Valak, and Valak is much smarter than Bathsheba, even though nobody dies. It hides behind the ghost of an old man, Bill Wilkins, so everyone thinks it's just a ghost. It blocks Lorraine's visions so she can't see the truth, throws Janet into a wall, and takes over her body completely. Ed has to fight his way in to save her. But Valak has one fatal weakness. If you know its name, you have power over it, and Lorraine sends it back to Hell just by saying it. It's smarter than you, and it will fool everyone you call for help, so your only chance is to learn its name. Survival rate, fifty-five percent.

## s06 #5 The Conjuring: Last Rites (2025), 45%, Better Run

Number five, Last Rites. This demon is stronger than that Valak, and it's the only one that has ever killed a Warren. It lives inside an old mirror, and in nineteen sixty-four, Judy Warren was born dead because of it, until Lorraine prayed her back to life. Twenty-two years later, it comes back for her. It hides behind three ghosts, makes a girl cough up blood and broken glass, drives the priest who tries to help to his death, gives Ed a heart attack, and takes over Judy until Lorraine has to pray her back to life a second time. Ed's exorcism doesn't even work, and only the whole family together, Ed, Lorraine and Judy, can break the mirror. Throwing the mirror away won't save you, and neither will a priest, so you only live if someone can pray you back. Survival rate, forty-five percent.

## s07 #4 Annabelle: Creation (2017), 40%, Almost Dead

Number four, Annabelle Creation. This is where the Annabelle demon began, and it's even more violent than the mirror demon, because it doesn't just drive people to their deaths, it kills them itself. It pretends to be Bee, the dead daughter of a doll maker, so her heartbroken parents let it into the doll. Then it goes after the orphan girls who move in. It throws Janice off her stairlift, wheels her into a barn, and takes over her body for good. Through her, it snaps the doll maker's fingers and kills him. Then it tears his wife in half, and twelve years later, it kills the couple who adopted Janice. A nun and five girls make it out, but only because the nun locks it in a closet, and the demon stays inside Janice. It doesn't want to scare you, it wants your body, and it never gives one back. Survival rate, forty percent.

## s08 #3 The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It (2021), 30%, Almost Dead

Number three, The Devil Made Me Do It. This one is the smartest of them all, because it doesn't have to kill you itself, it makes you do it. A witch hides a cursed charm in your home, and from then on, the killer is you. Arne Johnson stabs his landlord twenty-two times. A girl named Jessica stabs her best friend Katie twenty-two times, and then dies too. The witch kills her own father, and turns Ed against Lorraine until he nearly kills her himself. And when the witch fails, her own demon twists her body until she's dead. You can't fight it and you can't run, because the hands holding the knife are yours, and only smashing the witch's altar can break it. Survival rate, thirty percent.

## s09 #2 The Nun II (2023), 12%, Slaughtered

Number two, The Nun Two. This is Valak again, and this Valak is far stronger than any demon so far. It's hunting for the eyes of Saint Lucy, a holy relic that would make it even stronger, and it has been hiding inside Frenchie, the man who saved Irene, for four years. It lifts a priest into the air and burns him alive, snaps the neck of a kid making deliveries, kills the headmistress of a boarding school, and leaves a trail of deaths across Europe. Sister Irene only survives being set on fire because she comes from Saint Lucy's own family, and it takes a miracle, wine turning into the blood of Christ, to drive it out. Almost everyone it set out to kill in this movie died, and the one who walked out of the fire is from Saint Lucy's family, which you're not. Survival rate, twelve percent.

## s10 #1 The Nun (2018), 5%, Slaughtered

And number one, The Nun. This is Valak at its deadliest, loose from a doorway to Hell under an abbey in Romania. Before Irene even arrives, every nun in the abbey is already dead, and the sisters she prays with were never really there. It buries Father Burke alive in a coffin, and it drowns Irene until she stops breathing, and Frenchie has to pump her chest to bring her back. The Church sent a priest and a nun, they found the actual blood of Christ in the abbey, and even then, Valak gets away, hiding inside Frenchie. It wiped out a whole abbey, and the last nun alive chose death over letting it in, so if it's in your room, there's nothing you can do. Survival rate, five percent.
