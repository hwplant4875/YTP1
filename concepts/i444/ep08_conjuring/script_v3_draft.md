Title: Every Conjuring Demon Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving It

## s00 cold open

These demons will burn you alive in midair, bury you alive, snap your neck, make you stab your best friend twenty-two times, or turn your own mother against you. And sometimes, all it takes is a doll. We're ranking every Conjuring demon by your chance of surviving it.

## s01 rules

Here's the rule. Tonight, the demon from one of these movies is in your room, and it wants you. And every one is different. One is a doll that wakes up every cursed thing in the house, one wants you to give up your own life, and one wiped out an entire abbey of nuns. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and possession, which is how completely it can take over your body. Put it all together, and you get one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #9 Annabelle Comes Home (2019), 95%, Easy

Number nine, Annabelle Comes Home. This Annabelle is the weakest demon of them all, because even when it gets loose and wakes up every cursed object in the Warrens' house, it doesn't manage to kill a single person. The Ferryman chases the babysitter, the Bride takes over her friend, and a hellhound claws through the roof of a car with the little girl inside. The doll turns up in her bed, and later the demon behind it tries to take her soul. In the end, two teenagers and a little girl beat it on their own, by forcing the doll back into its case and locking it. Even loose in your room all night, it will scare you half to death, but you'll almost certainly see morning. Survival rate, ninety-five percent.

## s03 #8 Annabelle (2014), 80%, Don't Worry

Number eight, Annabelle. This demon is more dangerous than the last one, because it doesn't just scare you, it hurts you, inside your own home. It sets your kitchen on fire and hurls a priest through the air at the door of his own church. But it has one big weakness. In this whole movie, it never kills anyone with its own hands. Five people die, but four of them die at human hands, and the only life the demon gets is Evelyn's, when she gives up her own life in Mia's place. It will burn your home and throw you across the room, but it has never taken a life by force, so you'll probably live. Survival rate, eighty percent.

## s04 #7 The Conjuring (2013), 65%, Don't Worry

Number seven, The Conjuring. This demon is far more dangerous than Annabelle, because it doesn't need a doll or a deal, it gets inside your body, and moving out won't save you. Bathsheba was a witch who gave her own baby to the devil, and she curses anyone who lives on her land. She stops every clock in the house at three oh seven, leaves bruises on you while you sleep, yanks the girls' feet in bed and drags one across the room by her hair, and on the very first morning, the family dog is found dead in the yard. Then she takes over the mother, Carolyn, completely, and makes her try to kill her own children. The Perrons only survive because Lorraine reaches Carolyn with a happy memory, and Ed does the exorcism himself. She will follow you, hurt you, and climb inside you, but in her own movie she never manages to kill a single one of the Perrons. Survival rate, sixty-five percent.

## s05 #6 The Conjuring 2 (2016), 55%, Close Call

Number six, The Conjuring Two. This is the movie that introduced Valak, and Valak is much smarter than Bathsheba, even though nobody in the haunted house dies. It hides behind the ghost of an old man, Bill Wilkins, so everyone thinks it's just a ghost. It blocks Lorraine's visions and attacks her inside them, throws Janet out of her bed at night, and takes over her body completely. Ed has to fight his way in to save her. But Valak has one fatal weakness. If you know its name, you have power over it, and Lorraine sends it back to Hell just by saying it. It's smarter than you, and it will fool everyone you call for help, but in its whole movie it never kills a single person, and the moment someone says its name, it's sent straight back to Hell. Survival rate, fifty-five percent.

## s06 #5 The Conjuring: Last Rites (2025), 45%, Better Run

Number five, Last Rites. This demon is stronger than that Valak, and it's the only one that has ever left a Warren dead, even if only for a moment. It lives inside an old mirror, and in nineteen sixty-four, Judy Warren was born dead because of it, until Ed and Lorraine prayed her back to life. Twenty-two years later, it comes back for her. It hides behind three ghosts, makes a girl cough up blood and broken glass, drives the priest who tries to help to his death, locks Lorraine in the basement, and takes over Judy and makes her hang herself, until Ed cuts her down and she's brought back a second time. Ed's exorcism doesn't even work, and only the whole family together, Ed, Lorraine and Judy, can break the mirror. Throwing the mirror away won't save you, and neither will a priest, but it only takes two lives in its whole movie, and the one it possessed came back, so you've got close to an even chance. Survival rate, forty-five percent.

## s07 #4 The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It (2021), 35%, Almost Dead

Number four, The Devil Made Me Do It. This one is smarter than the mirror demon, because it doesn't have to kill you itself, it makes you do it. A witch hides a cursed charm in your home, and from then on, the killer is you. Arne Johnson stabs his landlord twenty-two times. A girl named Jessica stabs her best friend Katie twenty-two times, and then dies too. The witch kills her own father, and turns Ed against Lorraine until he nearly kills her himself. And when the witch fails, her own demon twists her body until she's dead. You can't fight it and you can't run, because the hands holding the knife are yours, and only smashing the witch's altar can break it. Survival rate, thirty-five percent.

## s08 #3 Annabelle: Creation (2017), 25%, Slaughtered

Number three, Annabelle Creation. This is where the Annabelle demon began, and it's even more violent than the witch's curse, because it doesn't need anyone else's hands, it kills people itself. It pretends to be Bee, the dead daughter of a doll maker, so her heartbroken parents let it into the doll. Then it goes after the orphan girls who move in. It comes for Janice on her stairlift and leaves her in a wheelchair, then wheels her into a barn, and takes over her body for good. Through her, it snaps the doll maker's fingers and kills him. Then it tears his wife in half, and twelve years later, through Janice and her cultist boyfriend, it kills the couple who adopted her. A nun and five girls make it out, but only because the nun locks it in a closet, and the demon stays inside Janice. It doesn't want to scare you, it wants your body, and it never gives one back. Survival rate, twenty-five percent.

## s09 #2 The Nun (2018), 12%, Slaughtered

Number two, The Nun. This is Valak loose from a doorway to Hell under an abbey in Romania. Before Irene even arrives, every nun in the abbey is already dead, and the sisters she prays with were never really there. It buries Father Burke alive in a coffin, and it drowns Irene until she stops breathing, and Frenchie has to pump her chest to bring her back. The Church sent a priest and a nun, they found the actual blood of Christ in the abbey, and even then, Valak gets away, hiding inside Frenchie. It wiped out a whole abbey, and the three people who lived through it only made it because they found the actual blood of Christ, so alone in your room, you're almost certainly next. Survival rate, twelve percent.

## s10 #1 The Nun II (2023), 5%, Slaughtered

And number one, The Nun Two. This is Valak at its strongest, stronger than any demon on this list. It's hunting for the eyes of Saint Lucy, a holy relic that would make it even stronger, and it has been hiding inside Frenchie, the man who saved Irene, for four years. It lifts a priest into the air and burns him alive, snaps the neck of a kid making deliveries, and kills the headmistress of a boarding school. Sister Irene only survives being set on fire because she comes from Saint Lucy's own family, and it takes a miracle, wine turning into the blood of Christ, to drive it out. It burned a priest alive in midair, and the only person who ever walked out of its fire was a descendant of Saint Lucy, so for anyone else in that room, the fire wins. Survival rate, five percent.
