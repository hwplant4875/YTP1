## Description (YouTube Studio > Customization > Basic info)
Could you survive a horror movie villain?

Horror Ranked scores the most famous killers, monsters and demons in horror on strength, intelligence, kill count and deception, then turns it all into one number: your chance of surviving them. Every episode ends with a full tier list, from Easy to Slaughtered.

Michael Myers, Jason Voorhees, every Ghostface, and next up Freddy and Pinhead. Tell us who to rank next in the comments.

New rankings every week. Subscribe and see where your favorite villain lands.

Clips are used for commentary and ranking. All film rights belong to their owners.
