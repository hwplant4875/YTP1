## s00 cold open
This Pinhead will tear straight through your flesh with hooked chains. This one will peel your skin off while you're still alive. This one will turn you into one of his Cenobites. This one will trap you in your own personal Hell, forever. And they all do it calmly. We're ranking every Pinhead by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 rules
Here's the rule. You just solved the puzzle box, and that movie's Pinhead is now standing in your room. We score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how merciless he is. That last one decides whether you can bargain your way out. Put it all together, and you get one number: your survival rate.

## s04 #11 Hellbound: Hellraiser II (1988), 85%, Easy
Number eleven: Hellbound. Doctor Channard forces a young patient, Tiffany, to solve the box for him. The Cenobites spare her, for now, because the desire that called them was his, not hers. Then Kirsty, who survived the first movie, shows Pinhead an old photo, and he remembers he was once human, a British army captain named Elliot Spencer. That's when Channard arrives, now a Cenobite himself. Pinhead and his Cenobites turn their chains on him, but his tentacles slice right through them. He cuts the Cenobites down one by one, then turns his power on Pinhead himself. That famous face burns away in a blast of light, and for a moment he's Elliot Spencer again, human, until Channard's blade finishes him. Kirsty and Tiffany walk out of Hell alive. Survival rate: eighty-five percent.

## s05 #10 Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), 70%, Don't Worry
Number ten: Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. On a trip to Mexico, Nico solves the box and gets dragged to Hell. He claws his way back out by getting his best friend Steven to kill for him, then kills Steven and wears his skin. Back home, he makes Steven's sister Emma open the box, so the Cenobites will take her instead. They don't. They kill Nico's mother on the spot, and Pinhead refuses the trade. As they haul him off, Steven's dying father shoots Nico dead, so the Cenobites take Steven's mother in his place. Only Emma, the one who opened the box, is left. Survival rate: seventy percent.

## s06 #9 Hellraiser (1987), 55%, Close Call
Number nine: the original Hellraiser. Frank solves the box, and the Cenobites rip him apart with hooked chains. When Kirsty solves it, she thinks fast. She offers them a deal: Frank, who escaped them, in exchange for her life. They tear Frank apart all over again, then come for her anyway, and she only survives by reversing the puzzle and sending them back to Hell. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

## s07 #8 Hellraiser: Hellseeker (2002), 45%, Better Run
Number eight: Hellseeker, and Kirsty is back. Her cheating husband Trevor wants her dead, so he gets her to open the box again. But this time, Kirsty knows how the game works. She offers Pinhead five souls for her own. Then she kills three of Trevor's mistresses, then Bret, the friend who plotted with him, and finally Trevor himself. She walks away, but only because five other people didn't. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s08 #7 Hellraiser (2022), 35%, Almost Dead
Number seven: the twenty twenty-two Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. This box has six configurations, and each one needs a victim marked by its blade. Riley's brother Matt cuts himself on it and vanishes, and her friend Nora is flayed alive. Riley only gets out by stabbing her own boyfriend as the final sacrifice. His name is Trevor, yes, another Trevor, and he'd secretly been finding victims for the millionaire who owned the box. And when the Priest offers to bring her brother back from the dead, she says no. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s09 #6 Hellraiser: Bloodline (1996), 25%, Slaughtered
Number six: Bloodline, the one that goes to space. The family of the toymaker who built the box is cursed, and this Pinhead comes for them in two different centuries. In nineteen ninety-six, he kills the man who opens the box, then John Merchant in New York. In twenty-one twenty-seven, he and his Cenobites wipe out almost the entire crew of a space station. In the end, it takes that whole station, folded into a giant puzzle box full of light, to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s02 #5 Hellraiser: Hellworld (2005), 20%, Slaughtered
Number five: Hellworld. Five friends go to a Hellraiser-themed party. Pinhead takes Derrick's head off with a meat cleaver, Mike ends up on a meat hook, and a cop gets a spike through the mouth. Then the twist: the host, who blames them for his son's suicide, had drugged all five and buried them alive. Those kills were hallucinations, but Allison, Derrick and Mike really do die in their coffins. Only Chelsea and Jake are dug out. And when the host opens the real box, the real Pinhead mocks him, then his Cenobites tear him apart. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s10 #4 Hellraiser: Deader (2005), 15%, Slaughtered
Number four: Deader. In Bucharest, a reporter named Amy finds a cult that kills its own members and brings them back, all because their leader wants to become master of the Cenobites. He can't open the box himself, so he kills Amy and brings her back to do it for him. When she does, Pinhead slaughters the whole cult, then tells her she now owes the Cenobites, and that her father is waiting for her in Hell. Rather than be taken, she takes her own life. Survival rate: fifteen percent.

## s03 #3 Hellraiser: Judgment (2018), 10%, Slaughtered
Number three: Judgment. Here, Hell has a whole department called the Stygian Inquisition, where an Auditor writes down your sins and a jury hands down the verdict. Detective Sean, secretly the Ten Commandments killer, forces his brother and his wife to open the box, then offers them up. Pinhead refuses any deal. Chains rip into both of them and drag them away. Pinhead has Sean killed, and even lets his chains tear an angel apart, which finally gets him thrown out of Hell. Survival rate: ten percent.

## s11 #2 Hellraiser: Inferno (2000), 8%, Slaughtered
Number two: Inferno. Detective Joseph Thorne solves the box and spends the whole movie hunting a killer who picks off the people around him. Then his own therapist turns out to be Pinhead, and he learns he's been in Hell since the moment he opened it. There's no fight, no deal, and no way out. Just an eternity of torment. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s12 #1 Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992), 5%, Slaughtered
And number one: Hell on Earth. This Pinhead feeds on victims until he breaks out of the Pillar of Souls, and once he's free, he needs no box and follows no rules. He massacres an entire nightclub and turns some of his victims into brand new Cenobites, who then kill the police. Only Joey, with the box itself turned into a blade, can send him back. Inferno's Pinhead wanted one man. This one wants everyone. If he's the one standing in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.

## s13 outro
So that's every Pinhead, from the one who died remembering he was human, to the one with no rules at all. Which box would you be brave enough to open? Tell me in the comments, along with the killer I should rank next. And honestly, I went through every death in all eleven movies to get this ranking right.
