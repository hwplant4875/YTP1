## s00 cold open
This Pinhead will tear straight through your flesh with hooked chains. This one will peel your skin off while you're still alive. This one will turn you into one of his Cenobites. This one will trap you inside your own head forever. And they all do it calmly. We're ranking every Pinhead by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 rules
Here's the rule. You just solved the puzzle box, and that movie's Pinhead is now standing in your room. We score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how little mercy he shows, which really decides whether you can bargain your way out. Put it all together, and you get one number: your survival rate.

## s04 #11 Hellbound: Hellraiser II (1988), 85%, Easy
Number eleven: Hellbound. When Tiffany solves the box, the Cenobites leave her alone, because the desire that called them was Doctor Channard's, not hers. Then Kirsty shows Pinhead an old photo, and he remembers he was once human, a British army captain named Elliot Spencer. That's when Channard, now a Cenobite himself, attacks. His tentacles slice through their chains, he cuts the Cenobites down, and then he strikes Pinhead. Pinhead's face burns away, and he dies as the man he once was. Kirsty and Tiffany walk out of Hell alive. Survival rate: eighty-five percent.

## s05 #10 Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), 70%, Don't Worry
Number ten: Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. On a trip to Mexico, Nico solves the box and gets dragged to Hell. He claws his way back out by getting his best friend Steven to kill for him, then kills Steven and wears his skin. Back home, he makes Steven's sister Emma open the box, so the Cenobites will take her instead. They don't. Pinhead refuses the trade and takes Nico back, and his Cenobites kill Nico's mother and take Steven's mother too. Emma, the one who opened the box, is left standing. Survival rate: seventy percent.

## s06 #9 Hellraiser (1987), 55%, Close Call
Number nine: the original Hellraiser. Frank solves the box, and the Cenobites rip him apart with hooked chains. When Kirsty solves it, she thinks fast. She offers them a deal: Frank, who escaped them, in exchange for her life. They take Frank, then come for her anyway, and she only survives by turning the puzzle back and sending them home. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

## s07 #8 Hellraiser: Hellseeker (2002), 45%, Better Run
Number eight: Hellseeker, and Kirsty is back. Her cheating husband Trevor wants her dead, so he gets her to open the box again. But this time, Kirsty knows how the game works. She offers Pinhead five souls for her own, then kills three of Trevor's mistresses, his partner in the plot, Bret, and finally Trevor himself. She walks away, but only because five other people didn't. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s08 #7 Hellraiser (2022), 35%, Almost Dead
Number seven: the 2022 Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. This box has six configurations, and each one needs a victim marked by its blade. Riley's brother Matt cuts himself on it and vanishes, and her friend Nora is flayed alive. Riley only gets out by stabbing her own boyfriend Trevor as the final sacrifice, and he'd secretly been finding victims for the millionaire who owned the box. Then she turns down every gift the Priest offers, even bringing her brother back. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s09 #6 Hellraiser: Bloodline (1996), 25%, Slaughtered
Number six: Bloodline, the one that goes to space. The family of the toymaker who built the box is cursed, and this Pinhead comes for them in two different centuries. In 1996 he kills John Merchant in New York. In the year 2127 he wipes out almost the entire crew of a space station. In the end, it takes that whole station, folded into a giant puzzle box full of light, to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s02 #5 Hellraiser: Hellworld (2005), 20%, Slaughtered
Number five: Hellworld. Five friends go to a Hellraiser themed party. Pinhead takes Derrick's head off with a meat cleaver, Mike ends up on a meat hook, and a cop gets a spike through the mouth. Then the twist: the host, who blames them for his son's suicide, had drugged all five and buried them alive. Those kills were hallucinations, but Allison, Derrick and Mike really do die in their coffins. Only Chelsea and Jake are dug out. And when the host opens the real box, the real Pinhead mocks him while his Cenobites tear him apart. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s10 #4 Hellraiser: Deader (2005), 15%, Slaughtered
Number four: Deader. In Bucharest, a reporter named Amy finds a cult that kills its own members and brings them back, all because their leader wants to become master of the Cenobites. Amy is the one who opens the box. Pinhead wipes out the whole cult in minutes, then tells her she owes them, and that her father is waiting for her in Hell. Her only way out is to take her own life. Survival rate: fifteen percent.

## s03 #3 Hellraiser: Judgment (2018), 10%, Slaughtered
Number three: Judgment. Here, Hell runs like an office called the Stygian Inquisition, where an Auditor writes down your sins and a jury decides your punishment. Detective Sean, secretly the Ten Commandments killer, forces his brother and his wife to open the box, then offers them up. Pinhead refuses any deal. Chains rip into both of them and drag them away. He has Sean killed, and even lets his chains tear an angel apart, which finally gets him thrown out of Hell. Survival rate: ten percent.

## s11 #2 Hellraiser: Inferno (2000), 8%, Slaughtered
Number two: Inferno. Detective Joseph Thorne solves the box and spends the whole movie hunting a killer, until his own therapist turns out to be Pinhead, and he learns he's been in Hell since the moment he opened it. There's no fight, no deal, and no way out. Just an eternity of torment. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s12 #1 Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992), 5%, Slaughtered
And number one: Hell on Earth. This Pinhead breaks free from the Pillar of Souls with no box and no rules. He massacres an entire nightclub and turns his victims into brand new Cenobites, who then kill the police. Only Joey, with the box itself turned into a blade, can send him back. If this is the one standing in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.
