## 1. 틀리거나 오해를 부르는 문장

### s02 Hellworld (가장 큰 오류)
- **v5 대사:** "most of what happens next turns out to be a drug trip... Two of them walk out alive... he's not there for the kids"
- **줄거리 (환각 속):** 핀헤드가 데릭의 목을 고기 칼로 자르고, 바운드가 마이크를 고기 갈고리에 꿰고, 핀헤드가 경찰관 입에 쇠꼬챙이를 박습니다.
- **줄거리 (실제):** 호스트(아담의 아버지)가 다섯 명을 약에 취하게 한 뒤 관에 산 채로 묻었습니다. 앨리슨, 데릭, 마이크는 관 안에서 실제로 죽습니다(목을 할퀴어서, 천식 발작, 공포로 인한 심장마비). 첼시와 제이크만 구조됩니다.
- **진짜 핀헤드:** 진짜 상자를 연 호스트를 비웃고, 채터러와 바운드가 그를 조각냅니다.
- **문제:** v5 대사는 아무도 안 죽은 것처럼 들립니다. 우리 기준인 "상자를 연 당신"으로 보면, 실제로 상자를 연 사람은 그 자리에서 죽었습니다.

### s03 Judgment
- **v5 대사:** "Pinhead mostly stands back and watches"
  - **틀림.** 핀헤드는 거래를 거절하고, 상자를 연 데이비드와 앨리슨을 사슬로 꿰어 끌고 갑니다("Amateurs"). 크리스틴을 시켜 숀을 죽이게 하고, 천사 조피엘을 사슬로 찢습니다.
- **v5 대사:** "If you're not a sinner, you're not even on the list"
  - **틀림.** 핀헤드는 상자를 연 두 사람이 "상자를 연 대가를 치를 것"이라고 말합니다.
- **v5 대사:** "an Auditor reads out your sins"
  - **부정확.** 오디터는 죄를 받아 적는 서기입니다.
- **맞는 부분:** "Pinhead gets thrown out of Hell" (신이 지상으로 추방).

### s05 Revelations
- **v5 대사:** "he leaves almost as quickly as he arrives"
  - **틀림.** 엠마가 상자를 열자, 핀헤드 일행이 니코의 엄마 케이트를 죽이고 스티븐의 엄마 사라를 대신 데려갑니다.
- **살아남는 사람:** 상자를 연 엠마만 살아남습니다.

### s09 Bloodline
- **v5 대사:** "Pinhead hunts the same family across three centuries"
  - **틀림.** 1796년의 악마는 핀헤드가 아니라 앤젤리크입니다. 핀헤드는 1996년과 2127년에만 나옵니다.
  - 그 대신 맞는 사실은 이렇습니다. 상자를 만든 르마샹 가문이 1796년에 저주를 받습니다.

### s12 Hell on Earth
- **v5 대사:** "kills the police"
  - **부정확.** 경찰을 죽이는 건 핀헤드가 새로 만든 세노바이트들입니다(CD, 바비).

### s07 Hellseeker
- **v5 대사:** "Kirsty opens the box again, and this time she's ready"
  - **부정확.** 바람피우던 남편 트레버가 그녀를 없애려고 상자를 다시 열게 만든 것입니다.
- **맞는 부분:** 다섯 영혼 거래(정부 3명, 브렛, 트레버).

### s08 2022
- **v5 대사:** "stabbed by its blade"
  - **부정확.** 칼날에 베인 사람이 제물로 표시되는 것입니다.
- **추가할 사실:** 트레버는 몰래 보이트에게 희생자를 찾아 주던 사람입니다.

### s00 인트로 컷 (대사는 맞음, 장면이 로어와 안 맞음)
- **장면:** "You get turned / Into one of his" 아래 장면이 Hellbound에서 채너드가 세노바이트로 변하는 장면입니다.
- **문제:** 채너드를 바꾼 건 리바이어던이고, 채너드는 오히려 핀헤드 일행을 죽입니다. "핀헤드의 세노바이트"가 아닙니다.
- **교체:** Hell on Earth에서 핀헤드가 희생자들을 새 세노바이트(J.P., 닥, CD, 바비)로 되살리는 장면.

## 3. 고친 대사 (v6 제안)

**s02 Hellworld (#5)**
Number five is Hellworld. Five friends go to a Hellraiser themed party, where Pinhead takes Derrick's head off with a meat cleaver, Mike gets impaled on a meat hook, and Pinhead even drives a spike through a cop's mouth. Then comes the twist. The host, a grieving father who blames them for his son's suicide, drugged all five and buried them alive in coffins, so those kills were hallucinations, but Allison, Derrick and Mike really do die down there. Only Chelsea and Jake get dug out alive. And when the host opens the real box, the real Pinhead mocks him while his Cenobites tear him to pieces. Survival rate: twenty percent.

**s03 Judgment (#3)**
Number three is Hellraiser Judgment. This time Hell has a whole office, the Stygian Inquisition, where an Auditor writes down your sins and a jury decides your punishment. When detective Sean, the Ten Commandments killer, forces his brother and his wife to open the box and offers them up, Pinhead refuses any deal. Chains rip into the two of them and drag them away, Pinhead has Sean killed, and he even lets his chains tear an angel apart. That's what finally gets him thrown out of Hell. Survival rate: ten percent.

**s04 Hellbound (#11)**
Number eleven is Hellbound. Pinhead is still terrifying, but when Tiffany solves the box, the Cenobites spare her, because the desire behind it was Doctor Channard's. Then Kirsty shows Pinhead an old photo, and he remembers he used to be a British army captain named Elliot Spencer. A moment later, Channard, now a brand new Cenobite, cuts through Pinhead and his whole crew in seconds, and Kirsty and Tiffany walk out of Hell. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

**s05 Revelations (#10)**
Number ten is Hellraiser Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. On a trip to Mexico, Nico solves the box and gets dragged to Hell, then claws his way back out by getting his best friend Steven to kill for him, before killing Steven too and wearing his skin. When Steven's sister Emma opens the box, Pinhead flat out refuses Nico's deal and takes him back, and his Cenobites kill Nico's mother Kate and take Steven's mother Sarah. Only Emma, the one who opened the box, is left standing. Survival rate: fifty percent.

**s06 1987 (#9):** 첫 문장만 "Number nine is the original Hellraiser."

**s07 Hellseeker (#8)**
Number eight is Hellseeker. Kirsty's cheating husband Trevor wants her dead, so he gets her to open the box again, but she makes Pinhead a deal: five other souls in exchange for her own. She kills three of his mistresses and his partner in the plot, Bret, and then shoots Trevor himself, the fifth soul. It works, but think about what it took, five people had to die so one could walk away. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

**s08 2022 (#7)**
Number seven is the 2022 Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. The box has six configurations, and every single one needs someone cut by its blade as a sacrifice. Riley's brother Matt is flayed, and the Priest tortures Nora until she's flayed to death. Riley only gets herself and Colin out by stabbing her own boyfriend Trevor, who'd secretly been finding victims for Voight, as the final sacrifice, and then turning down every gift the Priest offers her. Survival rate: thirty percent.

**s09 Bloodline (#6)**
Number six is Bloodline, the one that goes to space. The family of the toymaker who built the box is cursed for helping open a door to Hell, and Pinhead comes for them twice. In 1996 he kills John Merchant in New York, and in the year 2127 he wipes out almost an entire crew on a space station. In the end, it takes a whole space station turned into a giant puzzle box full of light to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

**s10 Deader (#4):** 첫 문장만 "Number four is Deader."

**s11 Inferno (#2):** 변경 없음.

**s12 Hell on Earth (#1)**
And number one is Hell on Earth. Pinhead breaks free from the Pillar of Souls with no box and no rules, massacres an entire nightclub, and turns his victims into brand new Cenobites, who go on to kill the police. Only Joey, with the box itself turned into a dagger, can send him back. If he's the one in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.

**s13 아웃트로:** 첫 문장만 "So that's every Pinhead, from the one who remembers he was human to a demon with no rules at all."
