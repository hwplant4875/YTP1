## s00 cold open
This Pinhead will tear straight through your flesh with hooked chains, peel your skin off while you're still alive, turn you into one of his Cenobites, or trap you inside your own head forever. And he does all of it, calmly. We're ranking every Pinhead by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 rules
Here's the rule. You just solved the puzzle box, and that movie's Pinhead is now standing in your room. And every Pinhead is different: some play by the rules, some break every deal, and one doesn't even need the box. So we score each one on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how merciless he is, which decides whether you can bargain your way out. Put it all together, and you get one number: your survival rate.

## s04 #11 Hellbound: Hellraiser II (1988), 85%, Easy
Number eleven: Hellbound. Of all the Pinheads, this is the one you'd want in your room, because he plays fair. When a doctor forces a young patient, Tiffany, to solve the box, the Cenobites spare her, for now, because the desire that called them was his, not hers. This Pinhead can even be reached: one look at an old photo, and he remembers he was once human, a British army captain named Elliot Spencer. And he can be beaten. That same doctor, now a Cenobite himself, cuts down Pinhead's whole crew, then burns that famous face away in a blast of light, and for a moment he's Elliot Spencer again, until the doctor's blade finishes him. Survival rate: eighty-five percent.

## s05 #10 Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), 70%, Don't Worry
Number ten: Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. This Pinhead is far stricter than the last one: he wants the man who called him, and nobody else will do. When Nico tries to trade a girl named Emma for himself, Pinhead refuses. When Nico's mother speaks out of turn, the Cenobites kill her on the spot, and when Nico is shot dead as they drag him away, they take Emma's own mother in his place. But Emma, who only opened the box because Nico forced her to, walks away alive. Keep quiet, want nothing, and you'll probably live. Survival rate: seventy percent.

## s06 #9 Hellraiser (1987), 55%, Close Call
Number nine: the original Hellraiser. Unlike the last one, this Pinhead will make a deal with you, and that's the trap. When Frank opens the box, hooked chains tear him apart. When Kirsty opens it, she offers them Frank, who escaped them, in exchange for her own life. Pinhead agrees, tears Frank apart all over again, and then comes for her anyway. With this one, a deal only buys you time, and to live, you have to solve the box backwards and send him back to Hell before the chains reach you. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

## s07 #8 Hellraiser: Hellseeker (2002), 45%, Better Run
Number eight: Hellseeker. This Pinhead keeps his deals, but his price is other people's lives. When Kirsty's husband Trevor gets her to open the box again, her only way out is a deal, five souls for her own, so she kills three of Trevor's mistresses, then Bret, the friend who was plotting with him, and finally Trevor himself. Most people couldn't kill five people to save themselves, and that's why this Pinhead is deadlier than the last. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s08 #7 Hellraiser (2022), 35%, Almost Dead
Number seven: the twenty twenty-two Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. The last one took five souls, but the Priest's box needs six, one for each configuration, and every victim is marked by its blade. Riley's brother Matt cuts himself on it and vanishes, and her friend Nora is flayed alive. Riley only survives by stabbing her own boyfriend, yes, another Trevor, who'd secretly been finding victims for the box's owner, as the final sacrifice, and when the Priest offers to bring her brother back from the dead, she has to say no. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s09 #6 Hellraiser: Bloodline (1996), 25%, Slaughtered
Number six: Bloodline, the one that goes to space. Running won't save you from this Pinhead, because he doesn't just come for you, he comes for your whole family. The family of the toymaker who built the box is cursed, so in nineteen ninety-six, this Pinhead kills John Merchant in New York, and in twenty-one twenty-seven, he follows the family all the way to a space station and wipes out almost the entire crew. It takes that whole station, folded into a giant puzzle box full of light, to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s02 #5 Hellraiser: Hellworld (2005), 20%, Slaughtered
Number five: Hellworld. This Pinhead barely needs to show up. A grieving father drugs five friends and buries them alive, and in their nightmare, Pinhead picks them off one by one, even driving a spike through a cop's mouth. The fear alone is real enough to kill: Allison, Derrick and Mike die in their coffins, and only Chelsea and Jake survive. Then the father opens the real box, the real Pinhead mocks him, and his Cenobites tear him apart on the spot. No chase, no deal, no second chance. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s10 #4 Hellraiser: Deader (2005), 15%, Slaughtered
Number four: Deader. This Pinhead promises a fate worse than death. A cult in Bucharest kills its own members and brings them back, because its leader wants to become master of the Cenobites, and since he can't open the box himself, he kills a reporter named Amy and brings her back to do it for him. When she does, Pinhead slaughters the entire cult, then tells her she now owes the Cenobites, and that her father is waiting for her in Hell. Rather than be taken, Amy takes her own life. Survival rate: fifteen percent.

## s03 #3 Hellraiser: Judgment (2018), 10%, Slaughtered
Number three: Judgment. This Pinhead doesn't even have to catch you, because Hell has already put you on trial. Here, the Stygian Inquisition has an Auditor who writes down your sins and a jury that hands down the verdict. A detective, secretly the Ten Commandments killer, forces his brother and his wife to open the box and offers them up, but Pinhead refuses any deal. Chains rip into both of them and drag them away, and the jury finds the killer guilty too. In this Hell, everyone gets judged. Survival rate: ten percent.

## s11 #2 Hellraiser: Inferno (2000), 8%, Slaughtered
Number two: Inferno. This is the smartest Pinhead of them all, because you never even see the trap. Detective Joseph Thorne solves the box and spends the whole movie hunting a killer who picks off the people around him, until his own therapist turns out to be Pinhead, and he learns he's been in Hell since the moment he opened it. There's no fight, no deal, and no way out, just an eternity of torment. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s12 #1 Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992), 5%, Slaughtered
And number one: Hell on Earth. Every other Pinhead plays by some kind of rule, but this one has none. He feeds on victims until he breaks free of the Pillar of Souls, and once he's out, he needs no box and no reason. He massacres an entire nightclub and turns some of his victims into brand new Cenobites, who then kill the police. Inferno's Pinhead wanted one man. This one wants everyone. If he's the one standing in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.
