# EP07 Pinhead script v1: Every Pinhead Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving Him

11 films, ALL kept (owner 11/01 18:35: no film may be dropped, HD first, find a way). Basis: you solved the box tonight and this film's Pinhead came for you. s00 cold open (frame 0 = Pinhead 1987 close-up + centre caption), s01 scoring, s02..s12 = #11 to #1, s13 outro. Stats STRENGTH, INTELLIGENCE, KILL COUNT, NO MERCY (can you bargain your way out; replaces SUPERNATURAL, owner 11/03). Voice Adam, eleven_v4. Facts: Wikipedia plots (11/01).

## s00 cold open
Hooked chains tear straight through your flesh. Your skin gets peeled off while you're still alive. You get turned into one of his Cenobites, or trapped inside your own head forever, and he does all of it calmly. We're ranking every Pinhead by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 scoring
The rules are simple: you just solved the box, and this movie's Pinhead is standing in your room. Every version gets scored on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how little mercy he shows, which really means whether you can bargain your way out, and that gives you one number, your survival rate.

## s04 #11 Hellbound: Hellraiser II (1988), 85%, Easy
Number eleven is Hellbound. Pinhead is still terrifying, but when Tiffany solves the box, the Cenobites spare her, because the desire behind it was Doctor Channard's. Then Kirsty shows Pinhead an old photo, and he remembers he used to be a British army captain named Elliot Spencer. A moment later, Channard, now a brand new Cenobite, cuts through Pinhead and his whole crew in seconds, and Kirsty and Tiffany walk out of Hell. Survival rate: eighty-five percent.

## s05 #10 Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), 70%, Don't Worry
Number ten is Hellraiser Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. On a trip to Mexico, Nico solves the box and gets dragged to Hell, then claws his way back out by getting his best friend Steven to kill for him, before killing Steven too and wearing his skin. When Steven's sister Emma opens the box, Pinhead flat out refuses Nico's deal and takes him back, and his Cenobites kill Nico's mother Kate and take Steven's mother Sarah. Only Emma, the one who opened the box, is left standing. Survival rate: seventy percent.

## s06 #9 Hellraiser (1987), 55%, Close Call
Number nine is the original Hellraiser. Frank solves the box, and the Cenobites tear him apart with hooked chains so completely that he has to be rebuilt from blood. Kirsty survives the only way you can, by making a deal, handing Frank over, and then reversing the puzzle before they take her too. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

## s07 #8 Hellraiser: Hellseeker (2002), 45%, Better Run
Number eight is Hellseeker. Kirsty's cheating husband Trevor wants her dead, so he gets her to open the box again, but she makes Pinhead a deal: five other souls in exchange for her own. She kills three of his mistresses and his partner in the plot, Bret, and then shoots Trevor himself, the fifth soul. It works, but think about what it took, five people had to die so one could walk away. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s08 #7 Hellraiser (2022), 35%, Almost Dead
Number seven is the 2022 Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. The box has six configurations, and every single one needs someone cut by its blade as a sacrifice. Riley's brother Matt is flayed, and the Priest tortures Nora until she's flayed to death. Riley only gets herself and Colin out by stabbing her own boyfriend Trevor, who'd secretly been finding victims for Voight, as the final sacrifice, and then turning down every gift the Priest offers her. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s09 #6 Hellraiser: Bloodline (1996), 25%, Almost Dead
Number six is Bloodline, the one that goes to space. The family of the toymaker who built the box is cursed for helping open a door to Hell, and Pinhead comes for them twice. In 1996 he kills John Merchant in New York, and in the year 2127 he wipes out almost an entire crew on a space station. In the end, it takes a whole space station turned into a giant puzzle box full of light to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s02 #5 Hellraiser: Hellworld (2005), 20%, Slaughtered
Number five is Hellworld. Five friends go to a Hellraiser themed party, where Pinhead takes Derrick's head off with a meat cleaver, Mike gets impaled on a meat hook, and Pinhead even drives a spike through a cop's mouth. Then comes the twist. The host, a grieving father who blames them for his son's suicide, drugged all five and buried them alive in coffins, so those kills were hallucinations, but Allison, Derrick and Mike really do die down there. Only Chelsea and Jake get dug out alive. And when the host opens the real box, the real Pinhead mocks him while his Cenobites tear him to pieces. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s10 #4 Hellraiser: Deader (2005), 15%, Slaughtered
Number four is Deader. A reporter named Amy tracks a cult in Bucharest that kills its own members and brings them back, all because their leader wants to become the Cenobites' master. When Amy finally opens the box, Pinhead slaughters the entire cult in minutes, then tells her she owes them for opening it, and that her father has been waiting for her in Hell. Amy's only way out is to take her own life before he can take her. Survival rate: fifteen percent.

## s03 #3 Hellraiser: Judgment (2018), 10%, Slaughtered
Number three is Hellraiser Judgment. This time Hell has a whole office, the Stygian Inquisition, where an Auditor writes down your sins and a jury decides your punishment. When detective Sean, the Ten Commandments killer, forces his brother and his wife to open the box and offers them up, Pinhead refuses any deal. Chains rip into the two of them and drag them away, Pinhead has Sean killed, and he even lets his chains tear an angel apart. That's what finally gets him thrown out of Hell. Survival rate: ten percent.

## s11 #2 Hellraiser: Inferno (2000), 8%, Slaughtered
Number two is Inferno. Detective Joseph Thorne opens the box and spends the whole movie chasing a killer, until he learns his own therapist was Pinhead the entire time, and he's already in Hell. There's no fight, no deal, and no way out, just an eternity of torment. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s12 #1 Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992), 5%, Slaughtered
And number one is Hell on Earth. Pinhead breaks free from the Pillar of Souls with no box and no rules, massacres an entire nightclub, and turns his victims into brand new Cenobites, who go on to kill the police. Only Joey, with the box itself turned into a dagger, can send him back. If he's the one in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.
