# EP07 Pinhead script v1: Every Pinhead Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving Him

10 films (Deader dropped: trailer-only footage). Basis: you solved the box tonight and this film's Pinhead came for you. s00 cold open (frame 0 = Pinhead 1987 close-up + centre caption), s01 scoring, s02..s11 = #10 to #1, s12 outro. Stats STRENGTH, INTELLIGENCE, KILL COUNT, SUPERNATURAL. Voice Adam, eleven_v4. Facts: Wikipedia plots (11/01).

## s00 cold open
Solve one little puzzle box, and he comes for you. He can tear you apart with hooked chains, peel off your skin, turn you into one of his Cenobites, or trap you in your own head forever, and he does all of it calmly, like a priest reading you your sentence. We're ranking every Pinhead by your chance of surviving him.

## s01 scoring
The rules are simple: you just solved the box, and this movie's Pinhead is standing in your room. Every version gets scored on strength, intelligence, kill count, and how supernatural he is, which really means whether anything you do can stop him, and that gives you one number, your survival rate.

## s02 #10 Hellraiser: Hellworld (2005), 80%, Close Call
Number ten is Hellworld. A group of friends get invited to a Hellraiser themed party, and most of what happens next turns out to be a drug trip set up by a grieving father who blames them for his son's death. Two of them walk out alive, and when the real Pinhead finally shows up, he's not there for the kids, he takes the guy who opened the box. Survival rate: eighty percent.

## s03 #9 Hellraiser: Judgment (2018), 60%, Close Call
Number nine is Hellraiser Judgment. This time Hell has a whole office, the Stygian Inquisition, where an Auditor reads out your sins and a jury decides your punishment, and Pinhead mostly stands back and watches. If you're not a sinner, you're not even on the list, and by the end, Pinhead gets thrown out of Hell himself. Survival rate: sixty percent.

## s04 #8 Hellbound: Hellraiser II (1988), 55%, Close Call
Number eight is Hellbound. Pinhead is still terrifying, but this is the movie where Kirsty shows him an old photo, and he remembers he used to be a human soldier named Elliot Spencer. A moment later, Doctor Channard, a brand new Cenobite, cuts through Pinhead and his whole crew in seconds, and Kirsty and Tiffany walk out of Hell. Survival rate: fifty-five percent.

## s05 #7 Hellraiser: Revelations (2011), 50%, Better Run
Number seven is Hellraiser Revelations, with a different actor under the pins. Two friends open the box on a trip to Mexico, one gets skinned and the other gets taken, and Pinhead refuses every deal anyone offers him. But he leaves almost as quickly as he arrives, and Emma is still standing at the end. Survival rate: fifty percent.

## s06 #6 Hellraiser (1987), 40%, Almost Dead
Number six is the original Hellraiser. Frank solves the box, and the Cenobites tear him apart with hooked chains so completely that he has to be rebuilt from blood. Kirsty survives the only way you can, by making a deal, handing Frank over, and then reversing the puzzle before they take her too. Survival rate: forty percent.

## s07 #5 Hellraiser: Hellseeker (2002), 35%, Almost Dead
Number five is Hellseeker. Kirsty opens the box again, and this time she's ready, she offers Pinhead five other souls in exchange for her own, including her cheating husband. It works, but think about what it took, five people had to die so one could walk away. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s08 #4 Hellraiser (2022), 30%, Almost Dead
Number four is the 2022 Hellraiser, where Pinhead is called the Priest. The box has six configurations, and every single one needs a blood sacrifice before it opens again. Riley's brother Matt is flayed, Nora is skinned alive, and Trevor becomes the final victim. Riley and Colin get out, but only because Riley turns down everything the Priest offers her. Survival rate: thirty percent.

## s09 #3 Hellraiser: Bloodline (1996), 25%, Almost Dead
Number three is Bloodline, the one that goes to space. Pinhead hunts the same family across three centuries, kills John Merchant in New York, and wipes out almost an entire crew on a space station in the year 2127. In the end, it takes a whole space station turned into a giant puzzle box full of light to finally destroy him. Survival rate: twenty-five percent.

## s10 #2 Hellraiser: Inferno (2000), 8%, Slaughtered
Number two is Inferno. Detective Joseph Thorne opens the box and spends the whole movie chasing a killer, until he learns his own therapist was Pinhead the entire time, and he's already in Hell. There's no fight, no deal, and no way out, just an eternity of torment. Survival rate: eight percent.

## s11 #1 Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth (1992), 5%, Slaughtered
And number one is Hell on Earth. Pinhead breaks free from the Pillar of Souls with no box and no rules, and he massacres an entire nightclub, kills the police, and turns his victims into brand new Cenobites. Only Joey, with the box itself turned into a dagger, can send him back. If he's the one in your room, there's no deal to make. Survival rate: five percent.

## s12 outro
So that's every Pinhead, from a drug trip at a party to a demon with no rules at all. Tell me in the comments which box you'd be brave enough to open, and remember, some doors are better left closed.

## Fact notes
- Hellworld: Adam's father (the Host) drugs the five friends; Allison, Derrick, Mike die (ambiguous); Jake and Chelsea survive; Host opens the box, real Cenobites kill him.
- Judgment: Stygian Inquisition (Auditor, Assessor, Jury); Pinhead has Sean killed, kills Jophiel; God banishes Pinhead to Earth.
- Hellbound: Kirsty shows Spencer's photo; Channard kills Pinhead and the Cenobites; Kirsty and Tiffany escape.
- Revelations: Nico solves the box in Mexico; Steven skinned; Pinhead refuses Emma's trade; Sarah taken; Ross dies; Emma left.
- Hellraiser 1987: Frank torn apart by chains, resurrected via Larry's blood; Kirsty's deal (Frank for her), reverses the box.
- Hellseeker: Kirsty's deal = five souls (three mistresses, Bret, Trevor) for hers.
- Hellraiser 2022: six configurations, sacrifice each; Matt flayed, Nora tortured and flayed, Trevor final victim; Riley and Colin leave.
- Bloodline: 1796 / 1996 / 2127; Pinhead kills John Merchant; 2127 crew killed except Rimmer and Dr. Merchant; Minos station = Elysium Configuration light trap.
- Inferno: Pinhead revealed as Dr. Paul Gregory; Thorne faces eternal torment.
- Hell on Earth: Pillar of Souls, nightclub massacre, kills police, new Cenobites (J.P., Terri, barman, DJ, Doc); Joey stabs him with the box-dagger.
