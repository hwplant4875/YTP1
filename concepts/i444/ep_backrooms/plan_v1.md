## Format (new, not the ranking template)
- Title: **How Long Would You Survive in the Backrooms?** Others: "Five People Entered the Backrooms and Only One Came Out", "Could You Survive the Backrooms Movie?"
- One continuous second-person journey ("you") through the film's real events, in the order they happen. No rank cards, no stat cards, no tier labels.
- One big number carries the video: **YOUR SURVIVAL**, shown big and clean whenever it drops, never as a drawn gauge. It falls with every choice the characters make.
- Each stage ends with the one rule that would have saved you, and the outro asks the viewer at which stage they would have turned back (easy comment prompt).
- Cold open keeps the channel's fast word-tag style but with a new twist line: "Five people walk into the Backrooms in this movie. One walks out. And even she never goes home."
- Fewer captions (owner trend 11/6): only the survival number, key words of the cold open and short stage words.
- Facts are judged only on what happens in the film (owner rule); Kane Pixels' original videos are background only.
