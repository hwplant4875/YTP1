# Halloween special script v1: Every Halloween Night Horror Movie Ranked by Your Chance of Surviving It

Same build as EP03: s00 cold open, s01 straight onto the stat card, s02..s11 = #10 to #1, s12 outro.
Stats: STRENGTH, INTELLIGENCE, KILL COUNT, SUPERNATURAL. Voice Adam (s3TPKV1kjDlVtZbl4Ksh), eleven_v4.
Kill claims checked against each film's Wikipedia plot section (10/31).

## s00 cold open
Tonight is the one night a year when a mask doesn't look suspicious, a scream doesn't sound like an emergency, and a knock on the door could be anyone. So if you had to spend Halloween night inside a horror movie, which one would you actually walk out of? We're ranking ten of them.

## s01 scoring
Every movie gets scored on its killer: strength, intelligence, kill count, and how supernatural it is, which is basically whether you can kill it at all. Put it together and you get one number, your survival rate for the night.

## s02 #10 Hocus Pocus (1993), 97%, Easy
Number ten is Hocus Pocus. Yes, three witches come back from the dead to steal the lives of every child in Salem, but on the Halloween night we actually see, they don't kill a single person. They get outsmarted by a few teenagers, a talking cat and a zombie, and the sun comes up before they can do anything. Survival rate: ninety-seven percent, unless you light the black flame candle.

## s03 #9 Totally Killer (2023), 80%, Don't Worry
Number nine is Totally Killer. The Sweet Sixteen Killer stabs each victim sixteen times on her sixteenth birthday, which is horrifying, but it also means he has a very specific list, and if you're not a sixteen-year-old girl in that one friend group, you're probably fine. Survival rate: eighty percent.

## s04 #8 Trick 'r Treat (2007), 62%, Close Call
Number eight is Trick 'r Treat. Sam, the little guy in the burlap sack, only comes for people who break the rules of Halloween, like blowing out a jack-o'-lantern before midnight. Follow the rules and he'll let you go. The problem is everyone else in that town, a principal poisoning candy, werewolves looking for dates, and drowned kids coming back from the quarry. Survival rate: sixty-two percent.

## s05 #7 Hell Fest (2018), 45%, Better Run
Number seven is Hell Fest. This killer has no powers at all, he's just a man in a mask, but he's hiding in a horror park full of people in masks, where every scream sounds like part of the show. He kills Gavin, Asher, Taylor and Quinn, and even after the police show up, he just walks out and drives home. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s06 #6 Haunt (2019), 35%, Almost Dead
Number six is Haunt. A group of friends pay to go into an extreme haunted house, and the actors inside aren't acting. There are six masked killers, the rooms are traps, and the doors lock behind you. Only two people make it out alive. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s07 #5 Halloween (1978), 30%, Almost Dead
Number five is the original Halloween. Michael Myers comes home after fifteen years and spends the night stalking babysitters. He kills Annie, Bob and Lynda, and when Dr. Loomis shoots him six times, he falls off the balcony, and then he's just gone. Survival rate: thirty percent.

## s08 #4 Night of the Demons (1988), 20%, Slaughtered
Number four is Night of the Demons. A Halloween party in an abandoned funeral home, a seance, and then a demon starts jumping from body to body, so the friend standing next to you might not be your friend anymore. By sunrise, only Judy and Rodger make it over the wall alive. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s09 #3 Halloween III: Season of the Witch (1982), 12%, Slaughtered
Number three is Halloween Three. There's no Michael here, just a toy company selling Halloween masks to every kid in America, and when their commercial airs at nine o'clock on Halloween night, every kid wearing one dies. The hero gets two channels to pull it, but the third one keeps playing. Survival rate: twelve percent.

## s10 #2 Terrifier (2016), 6%, Slaughtered
Number two is Terrifier. Art the Clown doesn't talk, he doesn't run, and he doesn't need a reason. On one Halloween night, he goes through almost everyone he meets, and the only survivor is found alive but so badly hurt she's never the same again. Survival rate: six percent.

## s11 #1 Terrifier 2 (2022), 3%, Slaughtered
And number one is Terrifier 2. Art comes back from the dead, kills the coroner, and spends Halloween night hunting Sienna and her little brother, and this time, he's clearly not human. Not even death stops him. Even Sienna dies before she comes back and finally cuts his head off, and he still isn't done. Survival rate: three percent.

## s12 outro
So that's ten Halloween nights, from three witches who couldn't hurt anyone to a clown you can't kill. Tell me in the comments which one you'd survive, and happy Halloween. Don't open the door.

- Totally Killer: 1987 victims Tiffany, Marisa, Heather (16 stab wounds each, on their 16th birthdays); 2023 Pam on Halloween night.
- Trick 'r Treat: Sam spares Kreeg after the candy bar ends up in his hand; principal Steven Wilkins poisons candy; Laurie and friends are werewolves; quarry bus children.
- Hell Fest: The Other kills an unnamed woman, Gavin, Asher, Taylor, Quinn; police arrive; he escapes and drives home to his daughter.
- Haunt: Mallory, Angela, Evan, Sam, Bailey die; Harper and Nathan escape; killers Devil, Clown, Witch, Ghost, Zombie, Vampire.
- Halloween 1978: Annie, Bob, Lynda; Loomis shoots him six times off the balcony; body gone.
- Night of the Demons: funeral home (Hull House), Angela possessed; Judy and Rodger climb the barbed-wire wall at sunrise.
- Halloween III: Silver Shamrock masks, 9 pm "Horrorthon" commercial; Challis gets two of three stations to pull it.
- Terrifier: Art kills Dawn, Tara, Will, Mike and others; Victoria survives, disfigured.
