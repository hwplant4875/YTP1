## s00 cold open
On Halloween night, you can be stalked, trapped, poisoned, possessed, hunted by a clown, and even killed by a TV commercial, and nobody will notice, because everyone thinks it's just a costume. We're ranking nine Halloween horror movies by your chance of surviving the night.

## s01 scoring
Every movie gets scored on its killer: strength, intelligence, kill count, and how supernatural it is, which is basically whether you can kill it at all. Put it together and you get one number, your survival rate for the night.

## s02 #9 Totally Killer (2023), 99%, Don't Worry
Number nine is Totally Killer. The Sweet Sixteen Killer stabs each victim sixteen times on her sixteenth birthday, which is horrifying, but it also means he has a very specific list. To be in danger, you'd have to be a sixteen-year-old girl in that one friend group, and honestly, what are the odds of that? Survival rate: ninety-nine percent.

## s03 #8 Halloween III: Season of the Witch (1982), 60%, Close Call
Number eight is Halloween Three. There's no Michael here, just a toy company selling Halloween masks to every kid in America, and when their commercial airs at nine o'clock on Halloween night, every kid wearing one dies. It sounds terrifying, until you realize the whole plan only works if you put the mask on. Don't wear it, don't go snooping around the factory where the androids crush people's skulls, and you'll probably see November. Survival rate: sixty percent.

## s04 #7 Hell Fest (2018), 45%, Better Run
Number seven is Hell Fest. This killer has no powers at all, he's just a man in a mask, but that's exactly the problem. He doesn't need a reason to pick you, you just have to be in the wrong maze at the wrong time. And he's hiding in a horror park full of people in masks, where every scream sounds like part of the show. When a girl begs Natalie for help, Natalie thinks she's an actor and points out her hiding spot, and when he kills Taylor on stage, the crowd thinks it's a trick. He kills Gavin, Asher, Taylor and Quinn, and even after the police show up, he just walks out and drives home. Survival rate: forty-five percent.

## s05 #6 Trick 'r Treat (2007), 40%, Almost Dead
Number six is Trick 'r Treat. Sam, the little guy in the burlap sack, only comes for people who break the rules of Halloween, but there are a lot of rules, and they're easy to break without knowing it. Blow out a jack-o'-lantern before midnight, skip the costume, or keep the candy to yourself, and he's at your door. And even if you follow every rule, there's still everyone else in that town, a principal poisoning candy, werewolves looking for dates, and drowned kids coming back from the quarry. Survival rate: forty percent.

## s06 #5 Haunt (2019), 35%, Almost Dead
Number five is Haunt. A group of friends pay to go into an extreme haunted house, and the actors inside aren't acting. There are six masked killers, the rooms are traps, and the doors lock behind you. Only two people make it out alive. Survival rate: thirty-five percent.

## s07 #4 Halloween (1978), 30%, Almost Dead
Number four is the original Halloween. Michael Myers comes home after fifteen years and spends the night stalking babysitters. He kills Annie, Bob and Lynda, and when Dr. Loomis shoots him six times, he falls off the balcony, and then he's just gone. Survival rate: thirty percent.

## s08 #3 Night of the Demons (1988), 20%, Slaughtered
Number three is Night of the Demons. A Halloween party in an abandoned funeral home, a seance, and then a demon starts jumping from body to body, so the friend standing next to you might not be your friend anymore. By sunrise, only Judy and Rodger make it over the wall alive. Survival rate: twenty percent.

## s09 #2 Terrifier (2016), 6%, Slaughtered
Number two is Terrifier. Art the Clown doesn't talk, he doesn't run, and he doesn't need a reason. On one Halloween night, he goes through almost everyone he meets, and the only survivor is found alive but so badly hurt she's never the same again. Survival rate: six percent.

## s10 #1 Terrifier 2 (2022), 3%, Slaughtered
And number one is Terrifier 2. Art comes back from the dead, kills the coroner, and spends Halloween night hunting Sienna and her little brother, and this time, he's clearly not human. Not even death stops him. Even Sienna dies before she comes back and finally cuts his head off, and he still isn't done. Survival rate: three percent.

## s11 outro
So that's nine Halloween nights, from a killer with a very specific list to a clown you can't kill. Tell me in the comments which one you'd survive, and happy Halloween. Don't open the door.
