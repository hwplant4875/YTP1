## 2. LAYOUT FACTS FOR THE 3D MAP

This episode supplies only the TRAP SITE. For the formation, plains and forest approach use ep 17-19 notes.

### 2.1 Formation / plains
- None shown in new footage. The 00:00-00:24 recap shows: a stone village with a windmill, timbered houses, wide green plains with low hills and a wide sky (00:17, 00:22, 00:23); horse riders in green cloaks; the Female's red legs stomping through houses. Treat this as ep 17-19 material.

### 2.2 The forest of giant trees (what ep 20 shows)
- Trunks: straight, vertical, bark deeply furrowed and sand-yellow/olive in the light, brown-grey in shadow; the trunk is far wider than a human: at 01:22 a soldier (about 1.7 m) stands on a log in front of a trunk that is roughly 4-6 m wide at the visible height (estimate from pixel ratios) and the trunks fill the full frame at 06:18 and 07:42. Root flares and buttress ridges are visible at the base (01:22, 05:12, 06:36). Working assumption for the model: diameter 6-10 m, in keeping with the 80 m+ trees noted in HANDOFF.
- Spacing: between trunks there is room for a horse column of 3-5 abreast (00:48 and 04:00) and for the Female (14 m) to run upright with the trunks well apart (00:28, 00:32); a gap of roughly 10-20 m between trunks (estimate). Light comes down in shafts; ground is dim; the forest is open at ground level (no undergrowth wall) with ferns and small shrubs at the base (06:11, 08:13) and a few fallen giant logs.
- Fallen logs: huge horizontal trunks lie across the ground, 2-3 m thick (01:22, 04:30, 05:12, 06:11, 08:13, 11:04, 16:13). Soldiers stand and sit on them: they act as walkways and as platforms for the launchers (11:04: Hange beside a barrel on a log).
- Branch levels: horizontal limbs ("branches") as thick as logs project out from the trunks at a low level (about 5-15 m up): soldiers stand on them with ODM (04:21, 20:56, 12:15). Above them: a closed canopy of dense leaf mass, with bright gaps (12:15, 05:23). A column of smoke rises out of the canopy, so the canopy top is clearly below the tip of the column (18:32).
- Canopy height: not measurable in the footage. The ceiling at 12:15 is far above the branches (the branch looks like a bridge between two trunks seen from below). Use the HANDOFF figure (80 m+).
- Light: warm yellow-green shafts, haze; dust and steam; the palette is olive/ochre/dark green.
- Clearing: the trap sits in a clearing of bare dirt/leaf litter with no undergrowth (00:28 dark trodden earth, 01:29 pale dusty ground, 21:02 footprints), wide enough for the Female with soldiers on both sides at about 6-10 m (01:29). There is a bright gap in the canopy above it (12:15).

### 2.3 The trap (ring of launchers)
- Launcher unit: wooden frame holding 2 columns x 3 rows = 6 round barrels; each barrel has 7 tube holes (6 around, 1 in the centre), "7 LAUNCHER" (info card 10:29; photo 04:12, 12:18). That is 42 harpoons per rack.
- Size estimate: a rack is about 2 m wide x 3 m tall (two soldiers stand beside it at 12:18 and are about as tall as 2/3 of it). It stands on its side, tilted upward, with a wooden shield/box beside it (04:12).
- Projectile: a long barbed arrowhead on a wire with a spring-loaded lock (card 10:33), fired with a lever ("Fire!" 10:40: a hand pulls a lever on the barrel side; 10:42: Hange fires with a hand-held trigger cord).
- Placement: racks stand in the trees on BOTH sides of the approach path (00:48: wooden posts at left and right edges of the track, the horses ride between them) and at several heights: ground racks, racks on logs (11:04), racks on branches. The wire web in 12:23 and 13:52 shows wires reaching her from at least 6 directions: left, right, front, back, above and slightly below. The Female ends up at the centre with wires criss-crossing about 2-10 m around her body.
- Operators: Hange at a launcher on a log (10:42, 11:04), Erwin on a log or branch with his hand up (11:53) to signal; Levi with an ODM unit on top of her head (12:28-13:20); two soldiers with blades flanking her at 01:29.
- Female position in the trap: stands on her feet, slightly bent, legs apart and straight, arms up and bent behind her head with hands covering the nape (00:56 / 01:29 / 04:06 / 12:28). She does not fall. Her skin and nape harden (white/crystal at 11:11). Iron pins stick out of her body at all angles (10:44-10:45, 13:04).
- Ring of soldiers in the trees: the 104th (Armin, Mikasa, Jean, Connie, Sasha, Reiner, Bertholdt, Krista, Ymir) and Levi's squad with Eren wait on trunks and logs at the outer ring, out of sight of the trap, close enough to hear it ("There's a real racket coming from inside", 05:07). Their horses are tied at the base of a trunk nearby (06:08). Distance from ring to trap: not stated. Sounds carry, so I would say within about 100-200 m (inference).
- Titans loitering in the forest: seen blurred in the background at 04:24 (3 individuals). They do not attack the soldiers.

### 2.4 Scale and appearance
- Female: 14 m (canon); in frame, a soldier on her head is a speck (12:28, 12:36), her head about 2 m wide. Hair: blond, medium-length bob/fringe, bleached white when crystallised (16:20). Skin: pale pink-beige with exposed deep red musculature on legs, arms and back; face is a flat angular mask with dark eye sockets and light grey-blue eyes; lips red. Hardened parts: white/translucent crystal (hands at 11:11, whole body at 16:20).
- Other titans in this episode: small to medium (3-7 m, no 15 m class), naked, brown-tan skin, all running (Abnormals; 14:22).
- Cloaks: dark olive green with the Wings of Freedom on the back; the shirt under is white, jacket brown, white cravat for Levi and Erwin; ODM gear brown leather straps and steel.
- Smoke: BLUE (dark indigo) column from the trap at 18:32-19:00; GREEN column, much higher and thinner, at 20:43 (fired from a flare gun held up on a branch, 20:38). No red, black, yellow or purple smoke in this episode. Interpretation (inference): blue is the withdrawal signal fired at the trap; green is the relayed repeat.
- Steam: after the titans die a thick off-white steam fills the clearing (17:04-17:56).

## 6. OPEN QUESTIONS / AMBIGUITIES

Suggest 10-16 racks around a roughly 20 m radius.
