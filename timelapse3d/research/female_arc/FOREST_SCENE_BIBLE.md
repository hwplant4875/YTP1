## B. World layout

**Frame F:** origin at the forest road entrance (barns plus a white-fenced yard, ep18 13:03); +y points along the travel direction into the forest; +x is to the right (the right flank).

- **Karanes gate:** about (0, -9000). The ruined old town (half-timbered houses, a windmill, an old stone tower) sits at about (0, -8500).
- **Formation dome:**
  - Size: about 800 m wide × 800 m deep, about 40 squads and about 120 riders.
  - Shape: an outer arch (vanguard plus wings) open to the rear; inside it, Line 2 "Command" with Erwin at the apex.
  - Eren and Levi's squad: in the centre column at the REAR ("Line 5-Center: Standby").
  - Supply wagons: about 10, at the rear right.
  - Speed: the whole dome advances at 10-18 m/s.
- **Plains:** rolling green fields with sparse normal groves (10-15 m), red-roof farmhouses, a village with windmills, a wooden plank bridge, and hills.
  - The Female's attack, the right-wing wipe and the village are at about (+600, -4000).
- **Forest:**
  - Footprint: y 0 to +3000, x -1500 to +1500.
  - Trees:
    - Trunks: straight, 5-8 m diameter, 80-100 m tall, bare for about 30-40 m.
    - Branches: thick horizontal limbs at 5-30 m.
    - Canopy: closed, dark conifer-like; its top is about 80-100 m up.
    - Spacing: gaps of 6-20 m.
    - Floor: fallen logs 2-3 m thick, ferns, dirt, golden light shafts.
  - Road: a winding earth/cobble road, 8-10 m wide, enters at (0, 0).
  - Trap clearing: about 40 m across at (0, +900), with a canopy gap above it. It is ringed by launcher racks (2 × 3 barrels × 7 tubes each) at ground, on logs and on branches, on both sides of the road.
  - Soldier ring: the 104th sits in the trees at about 150 m radius; horses are tied at trunk bases.
  - Exit gate: two huge trunks plus white railings on a paved stretch, at about (0, +1050) (ep19 20:45).
- **Squad fight area:** Levi squad's fight and Eren's duel happen in a swampy clearing (25-30 m) at about (+300, +1600). The head-kick happens on a fallen trunk nearby at (+380, +1700).
- **Levi/Mikasa rescue trunk:** about (+500, +2000).
- **Retreat:** back out of the forest's west edge and over the plain toward the walls.

## C. Trajectories

All movement is continuous and trackable, with no teleports (owner rule).

- **Female:**
  - Height 14 m. Runs a hunched sprint (on all fours when accelerating) at 15-20 m/s.
  - She enters from the RIGHT (+x) at about (+1500, -4200) on a dust trail and crosses the right wing.
  - Village at (+600, -4000), then she turns inward and follows the centre to the forest road.
  - Chase: about 20-40 m behind Levi's squad on the road, f7.
  - Trapped at (0, +900).

- **Levi and Mikasa:** come from behind her on ODM, then exit west.
