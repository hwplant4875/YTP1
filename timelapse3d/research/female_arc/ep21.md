## 0. What this episode is (and is not)

- This episode contains almost no wide or aerial shots. It is shot almost entirely at ground or tree level, with long lenses and close-ups.
  Formation shape, smoke signals, the wing attack and the harpoon-trap layout are NOT shown in this episode (those are in ep 17-20). The only glimpses are in the recap (0:00-2:57).
- Structure of the file:
  - 0:00-2:57 cold open = recap of ep 17-20 (departure, Female attack, forest, the TRAP and the Female eaten, Erwin/Hange talk). Contains the only usable view of the trap.
  - 3:00-4:40 opening credits (OP). SKIP.
  - 4:42-4:46 title card "#21 The Decisive Blow".
  - 4:48-22:21 the real episode.
  - 22:23-23:46 ending credits. SKIP.
  - 23:52-23:59 next-episode preview. SKIP. 24:04 card "#22 The Defeated".
- Real-episode story in one line: after the trap, the Scouts withdraw; the Female (shifted again) catches Levi's squad inside the forest, kills Erd, Gunter, Oluo and Petra; Eren transforms and loses to her; she bites Eren out of his nape and runs off with him; Mikasa and Levi pursue.
  Mapping to EP1_plan_v2: row 25 (trap and her being eaten) = recap only; rows 26 (Squad Levi wiped, Eren's rage shift, duel, Eren taken, Mikasa and Levi) = this episode.
  The "Eren is taken" beat is NOT the end of the Female's chase: the episode ends with Mikasa and Levi closing on her, with Eren still in her mouth/hand at 22:12.

## 2. LAYOUT FACTS for the 3D map

### 2.1 What the episode actually shows about the FORMATION / PLAINS (very little)
- Departure (0:02-0:16, recap): the Scouts leave Trost through the south gate arch (cobbled street, tall stone walls either side), riders in a tight multi-row pack (0:15: about 6 visible at the front, side by side). Not a wide-open formation yet.
- Plains (0:21, 0:42, 0:43): a grass plain with scattered single round trees and small pine clumps, a half-timbered village (0:41), low rolling hills and a distant tree line; sky: heavy clouds (0:21) or fair-weather clouds (0:42). In 0:43 the Female's feet are ~2x taller than a distant pine, consistent with ~14 m (inferred).
- NOT in this episode: smoke-signal colours, wing positions, the central supply wagons. Do not source them here; use ep 17-19 and the existing EP1_plan_v2 rows 22-24.

### 2.2 The Forest of Giant Trees (best data in this episode)
- Trunks are straight, almost branchless columns running off the top of the frame at 5:46 and 6:07. Bark: warm tan/orange where lit, deep brown in shade; vertical ridged texture (2:08, 21:40).
- Base shrubs and small pines at the forest edge are ~4-8 m tall (5:46, 6:07) and look tiny against the trunks (inferred: about 1/10 the visible trunk height).
- Trunk diameter (inferred from scale): about 3-6 m. At 21:40 Mikasa (1.6 m) hangs on a trunk face roughly 1/3 of the trunk's width tall: width ~5 m. At 19:28 Eren's titan (15 m) stands with a trunk beside it whose lower width is about 1/3 of his height: ~5 m. Canon knowledge (not seen here): giant trees up to 80 m.
- Spacing (inferred): at 5:46 the gaps between neighbouring trunks are about 1.5-3x a trunk diameter, i.e. roughly 6-15 m. This is enough for a 14-15 m titan to run through (18:12, 18:48, 19:28), but trunks block sight lines. In the fight clearings (14:34, 17:28, 17:55) the tree-free patch is about 20-30 m across (inferred from Eren's 15 m body filling about 1/2 of the width).
- Branches: trunks are mostly bare for many tens of metres; thick horizontal limbs exist (13:20: a limb ~1-1.5 m thick leaves the trunk horizontally; 9:36 shows squad members in the lower foliage with leaves, mid-height). Foliage clumps hang on the lower trunk sides (5:46: small leafy twigs on trunks at about 30-40% of the visible height). The squad flies at roughly 10-30 m above ground (inferred from 6:56, 7:36, 21:40 hang poses).
- Canopy: closed, very high; at 7:16 only small bright gaps; at 7:57 from above a dense dark-green conifer-like canopy under golden light.
- Floor: grass and low ferns/shrubs, mossy patches, bare dirt in the fight clearings (12:43, 14:34 shows a swampy, puddled floor with dust/spray when they run); in light shafts through the canopy (shafts angled steeply, e.g. 14:34, 20:11).
- Light: warm golden shafts, mostly from the upper left or the rear of the camera, hard-edged. Overall colour dark olive-green with gold highlights.
- A track runs along the forest edge/entrance: a wide dirt path (5:58) leads from the meadow toward the forest on the left of the frame, bending slightly right; in the forest an earthen path (2:08) winds between trunks. This is the "road through the forest" the squad rides on until they take to the trees. (Whether it is straight or curved overall is unknown.)
- The trap (recap 1:09-1:56; 1:42, 1:38, 1:46 at full size):
  - Cables: many thin taut black wires criss-crossing in a net from MULTIPLE directions around the Female (1:38, 1:46); roughly 30+ wires visible; each ends in a spiked harpoon/barb stuck into her body and legs (1:44: spikes in her red leg).
  - Female pose: standing upright, back to the camera at 1:42, both arms bent with hands behind her head on the nape, legs slightly apart, held up by cables.
  - Soldiers: at 1:42 the camera is low among three soldiers (dark green cloaks, ODM gear) standing/crouching in a ring around her, ~10-15 m from her feet; a tiny soldier crouches at her feet (bottom centre). Trap launchers are NOT clearly visible; 1:12 shows a radial burst of wires fired from all around.
  - Then titans rush in from the left of the frame (1:49-1:56), tearing at her in a flash of orange fire/steam.
  - Her legs below the hip are red muscle (no skin), hips/torso pale pinkish cream, arms red on the outside.

### 2.3 Sizes
- Female: about 14 m (canon; consistent with riders under her feet at 0:21 and soldiers' height at 1:42).
- Eren's titan: about 15 m (canon). At 19:28 he stands about 0.3-0.5 head taller than the Female (inferred).
- Horses (13:40 and 13:44 data cards, read at full 720p): about 160 cm tall, 450-500 kg; top speed 75-80 km/h (~21-22 m/s); sustained 35 km/h (~10 m/s); "just about the only way to outrun the Titans". The owner-brief 15-18 m/s gallop sits between sustained and top speed.

### 2.4 Colours
- Female: pale pinkish-cream skin on the torso, back and chest (12:22 back; 20:40); red muscle-fibre on arms and legs (0:43 legs fully red from the knees down; 19:36 the arms and thighs red on the outside); face scarred with dark rings round the eyes; hair: short light-blond bob, ~chin length, parted over one eye. Her eyes are pale.
- Female crystal hardening: translucent blue/white, gold-tinted when lit (12:32 wrist; 18:29 fist; 21:08 hand).
- Eren's titan: tan-brown skin with a darker brown shade; dark brown shoulder-length hair; green (turquoise) eyes (13:17, lightning arc: bright green); the shift lightning is yellow-white then a green arc (13:17).
- Scout cloaks: olive/dark green with the white-and-blue Wings of Freedom on the back (15:11); uniform: tan jackets, white trousers, brown leather harnesses.
- Mikasa: red scarf visible (20:16 bottom right, 21:56). Levi: black hair, white cravat.
- Steam: white; blood: dark red. Titan-shift lightning: yellow.
- Smoke signals: none in this episode.
