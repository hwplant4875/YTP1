## 0. What this episode is (and is not)
- Episode 19 contains ONLY forest material (plus a long flashback in a farm/castle yard). There is NO formation, no plains, no wing-wrecking, no smoke-relay chain, no supply wagons, no route bend, no Female-vs-column shot from the air. Those are episodes 17-18 (other analyses).
- It starts inside the Forest of Giant Trees with the Female already chasing Levi's squad, and ends with the Female CAUGHT in the harpoon trap, bound by wires, hands behind her neck. She is NOT eaten by other Titans here, the Levi squad is NOT killed here, Eren does NOT transform here (all of that is ep 20-21; the "next episode" teaser at 23:54-24:06 only shows Erwin, Mikasa/Armin-group, Levi, Eren and the bound Female).
- So ep19 maps to rows 24-25 of EP1_plan_v2.md (forest chase + trap) but the trap result differs from the plan: here the Female is captured (immobilised, alive); the "titans swarm in and eat her" part is later.

## 2. LAYOUT FACTS for a 3D map

### 2.1 Formation, wings, smoke relays, supply wagons
- NOT SHOWN in ep19. The only "formation" information is (a) the six-rider Levi squad, (b) the card below. Ask the ep17/18 analysis for the column.
- Info card (12:08 and 12:12, flashback): smoke signal gun = "small handgun that fires signal rounds using gunpowder; cylinders loaded with signal rounds are swapped for use". Colour code: **Red = Titan sighted; Green = new formation heading (course decided); Black = abnormal Titan sighted.** Yellow and purple are not mentioned here.
- Levi's gun at 1:30 and 3:19-3:24 is a grey cylindrical gun with a small spoked wheel on the grip (different from the long-barrel signal gun on the card); it makes a loud report (everyone covers ears) and white smoke curls out. No coloured smoke trail is visible in any frame. (The trap crew later fires on "Fire!".) I could not confirm a colour. Do not assume green.

### 2.2 Route and terrain
- Route in this episode: along a straight, wide, COBBLE-PAVED road running through the Forest of Giant Trees (cobbles visible at 19:34, 3:36, 6:56, 20:45). The road has no bends visible in the stills; light shafts come from straight ahead (6:20, a big white glare at the end of the road).
- Road width: six horses run roughly abreast at 20:45 with margin on both sides to white railings; about 6 horse widths + margins. INFERENCE: ~10-14 m wide. At 3:36 the 6 riders sit in a cluster ~3 across and 2-3 deep with room to spare.
- White wooden fence/railing along both edges at the forest exit (clearly visible at 20:45 and at the road edge at 0:55). The paved section at 20:45 looks like a causeway/bridge or park-style path.
- Trees (canon: up to ~80 m; this episode's stills):
  - Bare trunks with NO branches for tens of metres: trunk columns fill the frame in 6:16-6:20, 20:45; 21:06 looks straight up a trunk, canopy 60+ m up. INFERENCE: first branch level begins roughly 15-30 m above ground (the Scout on a branch at 20:24 is shown against a trunk with one big leafy branch).
  - Trunk diameter: at 21:35 two Scouts (about 1.7 m) stand in front of a trunk that is wider than the whole left half of the frame. INFERENCE: trunk diameter at least 5 m, likely 6-10 m, buttress roots with a toppled branch/log (diagonal, about 3 m thick) lying at the base.
  - Spacing: trunks are widely spaced with open understory; the road corridor between trunk columns is wide enough for light shafts and for the Female to run all-fours (about 13-14 m tall when standing, so arms/body fit between trunks). INFERENCE: trunk-to-trunk gaps of 15-30 m beside the road.
  - Canopy: dense, bright green, canopy top lit (blue sky pierces in shafts, 6:20, 19:57). Understory ferns/bushes at the road edge (20:45).
  - Ground away from the road: dark soil and leaf litter with shadow patches (3:36).
- A broad open meadow with hills in the distance appears only in the flashback (12:13-12:21: forest edge, a stand of trunks framing a meadow, steam from Eren's experiment ~ a few hundred metres off; 8:46 dry well in a meadow, wooden house, trees, hills). Not part of the sky route.
- Camera heights used by the anime for the forest: 1:11 and 3:36 are high angles from a branch roughly 15-30 m up (INFERENCE from foreground trunk and road scale), looking along the road.

### 2.3 The trap (20:09-21:46)
- Type: wheel-mounted harpoon launchers = rectangular wooden boxes with a drum face of 7 round barrels (6 around 1 centre), mounted on a spoked wheel for aiming (20:17, 20:30, 20:34, 20:41). Each fires barbed spears on wires. A second type: a 2x2 stack of drums in a single box (20:32).
- Placement (20:17, 20:21, 20:35): stations stand at the BASE OF TRUNKS on BOTH sides of the road; each has a tilted wooden plank shield/hinged wall in front of the crew; crew of 2 per station kneel and cover their ears; a leader raises an arm ("Fire!"). In the volley frame (20:35) two dark shield blocks sit at the bottom left and bottom right of frame with the Female silhouetted between them: launchers are close to the road edge on both sides, aiming across the road. Volley frames show wires and spearheads converging from all around her (20:31-20:33), so INFERENCE: a ring/arc of stations around the final stopping point, not just two.
- At least one Scout stands on a high branch at a trunk (20:24-20:27, upper right) holding ODM wires (likely lookout/signaller). A small triangular object hangs at the top of frame at 20:19 (wire anchor/lamp? unknown).
- Count: not stated. Visible: at least 2 stations near the road (20:17), 1 more in a flat shot (20:30/20:34), 1 stacked block (20:32). INFERENCE: 6-12 stations total.
- Timing: the six riders pass the line first; the Female is 1-3 body lengths (15-45 m) behind and leaps (20:17-20:19) into the line; volley 20:30-20:37 (about 7 s on screen); wires hold her standing, legs apart, hands behind her neck (21:42, 22:03).
- Result tableau: a dense web of taut wires (many nearly horizontal, at all heights) spread in a cone/ring around the Female; light shafts make the wires glint. She is still upright in the same place as the trap centre, at the foot of the forest road, with a toppled log beside the trunk where Erwin and Levi stand. INFERENCE only: at 21:35 Erwin and the second Scout stand roughly 10-15 m from her.
- The riders exit onto the stone road OUTSIDE the trap and slow behind a dust wall (20:45).

### 2.4 Sizes and speeds
- Female ~14-15 m (canon 14 m). Check from 21:42: she fills the full frame height, soldiers beside her are about 1/8-1/9 of her height, consistent with ~14 m.
- Female gait: normal run upright 0:24-0:52; at 19:14-19:30 she drops into an all-fours sprint (hands swing forward, back arched). INFERENCE: ~20-25 m/s, since she closes from tens of metres behind to touching distance within ~15 s of screen time while the riders gallop flat out ("Keep going full speed!"). Horse gallop 15-18 m/s as the owner states; riders here are stated to be at full speed.
- Riders: 6 horses at the exit (20:45). Dark bay/brown horses and at least one grey (20:17, 20:48).
- ODM Scouts appear in forest as lone fighters or at the trap branch; speed not stated.

### 2.5 Colours
- Survey Corps cloak: dark olive/forest green with hood, Wings-of-Freedom crest on the back (blue-white-silver). Jacket brown, shirt white with a cravat (Levi), trousers cream/white, white straps/ODM grey gear. In forest shade everything reads as dark green; in the 3:19 gun shot and the trap shot it is bright green.
- Female: pale skin (beige to a warm tan), EXPOSED RED MUSCLE on forearms/hands (red with white nail tips, 19:08), shins and calves (red), upper back/shoulder-blade patches and chest/abdomen vertical stripes (0:24, 1:11, 20:13). Short blond (yellow-straw) bob with a sheen; light blue-grey eye; red-ringed eye sockets; mouth with hardened dark greenish-grey teeth (22:13).
- Harpoon flash: warm gold/white sparks with long white wires (20:30-20:41). Dust after the volley: grey-brown cloud.
- Smoke colours known from the card: Red / Green / Black only.
