## 2. LAYOUT FACTS FOR THE 3D MAP

### 2.1 The Forest of Giant Trees (3:13-7:20)

[SEEN] Visible trunks: dozens of straight, bare trunks with no low branches, ridged bark, slightly golden in the god rays; undergrowth only as low bushes near the ground (5:51) and on side branches; forest floor dark and mostly clear, covered in leaf litter and low scrub (7:17).
[SEEN] Canopy: very high and dense; Levi at 5:51 is a small figure flying in a gap, the canopy is far above him.
[SEEN] Light: golden god rays, steep shafts coming from top-left or top-centre of frame, steam and dust catching the light (3:38, 4:04, 4:40, 6:21). Overall greenish-olive mid-brown shade; sky only visible as a tiny glow through the canopy (5:35, 5:51).
[SEEN] A very large fallen branch/tree leans across the frame at 6:38 and the 0:38 recap, giving horizontal "roads" through the trunks.
[INF] Scale: using the seated Female at 7:17 (about 8 m seated height) the trunk next to her measures about 4 m wide and the trunk behind her about 7 m wide. Using Levi (1.6 m) at 6:38 the trunk is about 5-6 m wide and the big branch he stands beside about 1.5-3 m thick.
[INF] So: trunk diameter about 4-7 m, gaps between trunks about 5-15 m (about 10-20 m centre to centre), a dozen or more trunks in view in every wide shot. Canon says 80 m or more height; a 14 m titan cannot rise above the first branches, so the first branch level is above about 15-30 m (cf. the Female hiding her head behind a trunk at 5:54).
[INF] Branch levels: the lower trunk zones (below about 10 m) are bare; branches, as the thick limb at 6:38 and the chase at 5:35/5:51, appear from about 15-30 m up, so ODM travels at roughly 20-40 m height; the canopy begins at an unknown height (canon 80 m+). Mark the exact numbers as open.
[SEEN] No clearing or road appears in ep 22. The forest "exit" is only mentioned ("before it gets out of the forest", 4:40). So there is no layout fact here for the ring of trap soldiers; that is in ep 20-21.

### 2.2 Terrain outside the forest (8:24-16:00)

[SEEN] Wide rolling grassland with gentle hills; sparse broadleaf bushes and trees; stands of dark conifers (pine-like, narrow, about 10-20 m); far low mountains and ridges (8:24, 12:08, 12:36, 13:53, 15:41). Several patches of bare, dry earth and tracks on the grass (12:21, 14:37).
[SEEN] The camp at 8:24 is at the foot of a conifer forest which is a normal-height forest, not giant trees; hills behind it (8:33).
[INF] So the camp is NOT right at the giant-tree edge; the giant forest is somewhere behind. Titans were "near the forest" (10:52).
[SEEN] No roads, villages or buildings in the plain: Erwin at 13:17 "I don't see any tall trees or buildings whatsoever", so ODM cannot be used, so they run for the walls.
[SEEN] Distant view at 19:34: wall with a central gate tower, a town in front of it with an access road and steps, trees on the left and right, a rock ledge in the foreground. [?] See Section 5.

### 2.3 The retreat column (12:08, 12:21, 13:03, 14:37, 15:41)

[SEEN] Not a tight formation: loose, scattered riders in a wide wedge. At 12:08 (aerial from behind and above) about 5 canvas-covered wagons (cream and brown striped canvas, two horses each) with about 25-30 riders in the frame. 13:03 (the red smoke shot) shows a denser cluster of about 50-80 riders and 5-8 wagons.
[INF] Spacing: wagons at roughly 20-40 m from each other; riders scattered 5-30 m apart both along and across the column; the whole column about 150 m wide and 100-200 m deep. These are guesses from perspective.
[SEEN] Wagon speed: info card 12:13: about 20 km/h cruising (5.5 m/s) on paved roads and bad terrain alike.
[INF] Riders flee faster than that: horse gallop 15-18 m/s (brief); Titans keep up ("At this rate, they'll catch us!" 13:45), so an abnormal sprint must be about 15-20 m/s, about the gallop speed.

## 3. CHARACTER POSITIONS AND MOVEMENT BY BEAT

Speeds: ODM about 15-25 m/s [INF/brief]; horse gallop 15-18 m/s; wagon cruising about 5.5 m/s.

Beat A (3:13-3:46) Chase begins.
- Female ahead, on foot, walking away from Levi and Mikasa (3:38 back view). She is slow ("exhausted") at 14 m walking pace, roughly 5 m/s.
- Levi and Mikasa behind her on ODM, in the trees, about 15-30 m back [INF from 4:36-4:40, where she looks about 20 m ahead of the blade]. They travel together, Levi in front (3:30 Levi in the foreground, 3:34 Mikasa).

Beat B (3:48-4:48) Plan talk.
- Both keep moving along her route while talking.
- Goal: reach her before she leaves the forest, which fixes her direction of travel as toward the forest edge.

Beat C (4:50-5:21) Mikasa decoy, Levi attack.
- Mikasa swings across the Female's front/side (4:54); the Female turns her head toward Mikasa (5:01).
- Levi arcs wide behind a trunk (5:08) and attacks from the rear/left of her (5:12: Levi is at her back, blades down).
- Female swings a hand (5:22).

Beat D (5:26-5:49) Cripple.
- Levi spirals around her limbs, wrists then legs (5:26-5:35). She goes down on her rear and slides against a trunk, legs extended and one hand covering the nape (5:42). Mikasa stays at a distance (5:43).

Beat E (5:49-6:41) Nape and mouth.
- She hunches against the trunk (5:54-6:03), hands on the nape. Levi crosses over, cuts the fingers (6:18-6:21), then opens her mouth and Eren comes out in saliva (6:26-6:41).

- Erwin on the outcrop (10:38) plus officers: standing talk, then ordering the retreat (11:45 "final decision"), column mounts.

Beat H (12:04-16:00) Retreat.
- Column moves along its direction at gallop; Mikasa rides beside the wagon with Eren; Jean and Armin on horse/wagon; Levi on horse next to the wagons; Connie, Sasha etc. on wagons (14:39 two soldiers on a wagon with blades drawn; 14:44 Levi on horse next to the wagon).

- Corpses are dumped from the rearmost wagon (15:13-15:22), the Titans slow down; Mikasa and Levi kill the pursuing Titans around 14:10-14:28 and 15:28-15:39 [?] who exactly (Mikasa at 14:14, Levi near 15:36).
- 15:49: the column, a tiny dot group on a distant plain.
