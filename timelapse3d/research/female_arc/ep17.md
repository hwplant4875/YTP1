## 2. LAYOUT FACTS FOR A 3D MAP

### 2.1 Long-range scouting formation (diagram frames: `ep17_05m00s_diagram.jpg`, `ep17_06m05_erwin_diagram_full.jpg`, `ep17_05m09_wagons_full.jpg`, `ep17_03m52s_full.jpg`)
- The diagram has FRONT at the top (arched vanguard) and REAR at the bottom. Squares = squads or riders.
- Counted from the 5:00 inset (approx. 39-40 markers):
  - Outer arch (vanguard + wings, a semi-ring open to the rear): ~16 markers. Top = 4 markers at the very front (a small arc of 2 + 2), going down to the two wing tips, which end at the same line near the middle height of the formation. The outer arch is NOT a full circle; it is a dome whose legs end level with the middle of the formation.
  - Second ring (rounded U-shaped lane, "Line 2" apex at the front, open ends at the rear): 10 markers; the apex marker at the front is Erwin (red marker in the 6:05 inset, "Line 2-Center: Command" = the apex of the second ring, directly behind the front arch).
  - Two thin inner lanes, one in each wing (left and right of the central column), 5 markers each.
  - Central column: 3 markers in a narrow lane in the middle (Line 5-Center: standby = rear marker). Eren and the special-ops squad (Petra, Oluo, Gunther, Eld) are at the REAR of the central column (red marker at 5:00-5:05 inset: "Line 5-Center: Standby", Eren shown with Jean-like and Petra-like riders).
  - Relay markers are tagged "Line 2-5: Relay", "Line 3-4: Relay", "Line 2-4: Relay" (row-column labels).
- Formation = "human radar": any titan sighted by the vanguard is signalled by smoke, relayed inward to Erwin in the centre, who then changes course by a green smoke arrow so the whole formation avoids the titan (diagram at 6:20-6:26: green arrow; 11:28: a black triangle shows the formation turning along a curved route).
- The 11:28 info card shows the formation rotated as a triangle-ish dome with an arched path behind it: the course CURVES (S-bend) away from the encounter.
- Supply wagons: covered wagons travel inside the formation at the REAR / rear-right (overhead shot: a column of ~10 wagons at the lower right of the formation; 5:09 shows a covered cart behind the riders on the right side). Wagons are boxy, small, with grey-brown canvas; horse-drawn.
- Overhead shot 4:49-4:51 (frame `ep17_04m49_aerial_full.jpg`): ~120 tiny riders in a rough grid of staggered rows; a dirt track runs diagonally from upper-left to lower-right; the wagons are at the lower-right (rear) heading toward the bottom-right edge, so the formation is travelling toward the BOTTOM of the frame in that shot? (inference: the wagon column is at the rear, so travel is toward the upper-left; the camera is tilted so that the top of the frame is ahead). The shot is stylised: rider spacing is ~4 rider-lengths there, much tighter than the 50-100 m the episode text implies. For the 3D map use "spread out, within sight of someone on all sides" (Erwin's lecture at 5:00-5:04: "stay within eyesight of someone on all sides", "fixed distances from one another", "extend survey and relay range as far as possible"), so spacing roughly 50-100 m is a reasonable choice (inference).
- Lines referenced: at least 5 lines front to back (Line 1 vanguard arch, Line 2 command apex, ... Line 5 centre-rear).
- Scale of the formation (inference): rider spacing 50-100 m, ~5 rows -> the whole formation is ~500-800 m wide and ~600-900 m deep. At gallop 15-18 m/s it advances ~1 km/min.

### 2.2 Route and terrain
- Departure: Calanes District (Karanes) gate (Wall Rose), a stone-paved ramp and street (1:00), ruined old town just outside the gate (2:56-3:50) with half-timbered houses, a windmill (2:56) and a stone wall/old tower (3:20). "The support squad will only escort us to the old town area" (3:52); beyond is titan territory.
- After the old town: open rolling green plain, sparse groves of broadleaf and conifer trees (10-15 m, normal size, not giant), isolated farmhouses with red-tiled roofs (4:32, 4:49-ish top right), hills and mountains in the distance in every direction, low sun and big cumulus (late afternoon light, long shadows).
- Dirt tracks cross the plain (4:49 shot, diagonal path).
- Large trunks and a tall tree stand at the field edge at 20:32 and 21:02 (a trunk several metres thick at the left edge of the frame next to Jean). This is the first hint of the giant-tree zone; real giant trees are in ep18.
- A tiny house at 4:32 right, a village at 4:32 left (half-timbered houses on a low rise), a lone tree on a rise.

### 2.4 Sizes and colours
- Female Titan: 14 m class (said by Ness/Armin, 9:43). Slim, muscular, human female proportions. Skin: pale pinkish-white on torso/back/arms with large areas of exposed deep red muscle on legs, arms, shoulders and parts of the chest; blonde pale short bob hair (to the jaw, parted in front), light blue-grey eyes with a glowing look when angry; sturdy, no genitals/breasts shown as in other titans except implied shape.
- Abnormal/normal titans: 10-15 m, naked, tan/peach skin, odd proportions (herd at 13:48: pot-bellied, bald or black-haired, running upright).
- Survey Corps: dark green cloaks with Wings of Freedom crest, brown jackets, white pants, ODM gear on hips; horses: bay/brown, some chestnut, Erwin on a white/pale horse (6:16).
- Smoke signals in ep17: RED = titan (normal) sighted (5:56, 6:56); GREEN = course change arrow (6:20, diagram); YELLOW = right-flank/Jean's signal at 13:36-13:44 (yellow trails fired from the right flank and a yellow beam rising near Jean; Armin says "from the right flank?"); black smoke for Abnormals is mentioned ("I saw a smoke signal for Abnormals", 13:16) but not clearly seen. Purple not seen in ep17 (open: check ep18).
- Steam: white from a dead titan (9:12 white column).

## 3. CHARACTER POSITIONS AND MOVEMENT (for a continuous sky track)

Speeds (inference): horse at gallop 15-18 m/s as in the owner's note; Female at full run (9:31-10:04): first seen at a long distance and on top of Ness/Sys within ~20 s, about 20-25 m/s; later (17:50-18:50) she "slowed down a lot" to roughly a fast walk/jog (inference 6-10 m/s).

1. Formation start (4:32-5:10): column leaves Calanes through the gate; riders fan out in the plain. Erwin Line 2-centre, behind the front arch; wagons in the rear; special-ops squad (Eren, Petra, Oluo, Gunther, Eld) in the central column rear (Line 5-centre). Levi's position is not shown in this episode (open question).
2. Red smoke / creeping titan (6:30-7:25): a recruit sees the titan, fires red; two other riders lure it and ride off with it crawling after them; they come back to formation (7:28 "Get back in position!").
3. Armin (7:44-8:04): rides alone toward the formation's centre while ignoring nearby people; Ness/Sys kill a normal titan (8:20-9:00) with Armin watching from horseback; he then rides near them.
4. Female entrance (9:31-10:08): she comes running from far (hills behind, left-of-centre at 9:32) straight at the right flank scouts (Ness, Sys, Armin's group). She passes them, kills Sys and mauls Ness's group (9:42-10:08). She stops before Armin (10:08-10:20), looks, stomps/clears the nearby riders (10:56-11:10), lifts Armin's hood (12:16-12:24), then runs off toward the formation's interior (12:32-12:40 she runs away, back view, into the plain).
5. Armin/Reiner/Jean (12:45-21:20): Reiner arrives on horse, Jean fires a yellow signal. Three riders gallop after the Female across the plain (16:00-17:50; she is ahead, running away from them), with Armin at the left, Reiner in the centre, Jean at the right when seen from behind (15:52-16:04: Reiner in the middle, Armin between Reiner and Jean at 15:52 sheets; positions vary - treat as approximate). They close in; Female slows; they attack her legs (19:00-20:00). Reiner kills/disarms her hand grip (20:40-21:10); Female stops and turns away.
6. End (21:20-22:20): Female walks/runs away; Armin is on the ground behind Reiner. Female heads away from the three (back view 22:00-22:16).

## 4. ORIENTATION CHECKS

- 3:16: "A 10 meter class approaching from ahead, on the left!" -> first titan is front-left of the column as seen facing the travel direction.
- 4:36-4:52 / 5:00 diagram: front = top of the diagram, so "right wing" = diagram right when facing travel direction.
- 9:20-9:31: "What's the right flank scout squad doing?" -> the right flank is the side where the Female comes through. The Female approach direction in ep17 is from the right flank (stated by Armin 13:48: "it seems we lost part of the right flank scout squad"; Female's approach direction "That's the same direction she came from" about the horde at 13:55).
- 13:36-13:44: yellow smoke from the right flank (screen right of Reiner/Armin who look right with backs to the camera) -> flank signals come from the east side if the column travels north? Cardinal directions are NOT given in ep17; use "right of heading".
- 13:56-14:10: the titan herd appears behind trees and "are somehow being held back for now" -> they stay on the right flank side behind the Female's route.
- 15:08-15:48: Jean: "mine had them (Eren's squad) around the front of the right flank" vs "the plans I got had them around the rear of the left flank"; Reiner: they are most likely in the safest spot of the formation = centre rear (Armin 15:40-15:48). So Eren's group is at the centre rear. The Female has crossed the right flank toward the centre rear.
- 21:56-22:16: she walks away, hills behind her (back view). Direction relative to formation not stated (open).
- Sun/shadow: low warm sun in front/side of the riders in many shots (late afternoon); shot direction not reliable for compass.
