## 1. Lore summary of the beats, in order, with evidence

1. **HQ under siege.** The supply HQ is a big pale-stone complex on a slight rise, with an open paved ring around it and dense terracotta roofs beyond. About 6-8 titans crowd its walls. Some cling to and climb the keep's lower crenellated block, and one climbs the clock-tower corner `[EV]` ep07 04:38.3 F07/000006673 (misty aerial; same composition as ep08 10:23). From the town it is the tallest silhouette, a crenellated keep with a clock/bell tower, under an overcast sky with gas streaks ("the Titans swarmed, making resupplying impossible") `[EV]` ep07 05:50.2 F07/000008397.
   - It stands on a rise with Wall Rose directly behind it, and titans cling to the walls of its lower crenellated block `[EV]` ep08 06:29.2 F08/000009331.
   - Seen from far down a long straight street, the Attack Titan looks toward the HQ on its rise `[EV]` 06:33.2 F08/000009427.
2. **Titans packed in the streets near the HQ.** 5-8 titans (3-7 m) stand shoulder to shoulder with their backs to the street, faces to a pale-stone wall with Gothic windows. There is one blood smear on the wall, and they are feeding ("It's our chance now that some of them are distracted", 05:38.2 F08/000008108) `[EV]` ep08 05:34.2 F08/000008012.
   - That building has **terracotta** roofs and dormers, so it is a neighbouring building, not the slate-roofed HQ `[INF]`. Use it as "titans clustered at the foot of a facade", not as proof that titans press against the HQ itself.
3. **Jean's squad flies to the HQ and crashes in.** Cadets cross the roofs by ODM, with titans running after them down a street (06:37.7, 06:38.5 sheet_11/12). Titans of 8-10 m stand in the streets with their upper bodies above the 3-4 storey roofs `[EV]` ep08 07:04.6 F08/000010180. Jean lands on one of the keep's front corner turrets (07:17.1 F08/000010479). The keep-front view from there is the owner's keep ref (07:18.5). He then smashes through a tall arched window with diamond-pane leading, and glass explodes inward `[EV]` ep08 07:25.5-07:29.5 (sheet_12, F08/000010682).
4. **Titans break into the upper hall; the Attack Titan's fist arrives.**
   - At 08:25.0 a titan's head smashes through the end wall of the long upper hall (bright burst, then a dust cloud at 08:26.7) `[EV]` sheet_13/14.
   - **Two titans climb over the keep's crenellated parapet** onto the roof walk `[EV]` 08:29.7 F08/000012221. Cadets flee the debris ("Get inside!" 08:32.8 F08/000012295).
   - The same two titans (one long-haired blond, one short-haired) peer in grinning through the **ragged, rubble-rimmed hole** about 3-4 m wide `[EV]` 09:00.4 F08/000012957.
   - At 09:15.7 (F08/000013324) **the Attack Titan's fist punches one of them in the face** through the hole from outside `[EV]`. The previous spec claim that "a titan punches the hole at 09:15.9" was wrong: the hole already exists, and the fist is Eren's.
5. **The Attack Titan tackles them off the keep.**
   - He is airborne beside the keep's tall facade with both titans in his arms, flying across the pale plaza `[EV]` ep08 09:16.9 F08/000013352. Two more titans stand on the plaza at the left.
   - The **round-arched arcade** in that frame is the ground floor of the range **adjoining** the keep (right of it), not the keep itself. A slender pale bell tower with an orange-brown cap stands in the background.
   - Two titans land upside down in front of a steep slate-roofed range with dormers, pilaster strips and arched windows `[EV]` 09:20.4 F08/000013435. Eren's titan stands beside the crenellated keep `[EV]` 09:22.8 F08/000013493.
   - **Armin, Mikasa and Connie crash in through a leaded window** just as their gas runs out ("Whew, it just ran out") `[EV]` ep08 09:30.8-09:36.0 (sheet_15).
   - The rubble of the hole lies at window level, and the Attack Titan's face looks in through it `[EV]` 09:40.0-09:54.1 (sheet_15).
6. **The rampage on the plaza.**
   - The best aerial of the HQ is ep08 10:23.0 F08/000014937 (= 10:27.0 F08/000015033). It shows a **quadrangle**: a crenellated lower keep block with a raised inner block carrying the dark punched hole on its plaza face, the tall clock tower at the keep's back corner, a 3-storey + slate-mansard range with a ground arcade adjoining the keep, a courtyard and back ranges.

## 3. Set dressing and materials

- **Keep** (owner ref keep_front = F08/000010514; F08/000030749; owner ref wing).
  - Warm sandstone ashlar #cdb48a with visible block courses (texture or trim sheet) and soot/grime streaks #8f8068 running down from the parapets.
  - Tier 1: plain ashlar walls with few openings. Square corner turrets with 2 narrow slit windows high up. Crenellated parapet with merlons 1.2 m tall x 0.8 m wide and 0.4 m gaps. Roof walk of 1 m pale flagstones #cfc6b0.
  - Tier 2 south face: the **row of 7 round-arched windows**, deep-set and dark #2e2a27, below its own crenellation. 2 front + 2 back small turrets with pyramid caps.
  - Turret pyramid roofs: **slate blue-grey #5a6b7e** (owner, HANDOFF 13:49: "only turret roofs slate blue-grey"; BRIEF: "slate-blue pyramid roofs"). The anime's green-teal in the 21:22.5 dissolve is overridden.
  - Clock/bell tower: plain ashlar, a belfry stage with one large arched opening per side, a white clock face (Ø 3 m) on the south side of the top stage, and a small pedimented cap (F08/000009331 crop, owner ref wing).
  - Damage: one long black crack down the keep's plaza (south) face near where it meets the wing (20:23.3 F08/000029330). From t = 0 a **ragged, rubble-rimmed 3-4 m hole** sits in tier 2's south face just above the roof walk, at Z 29-33. It is the dark spot on the raised block in F08/000014937, with rubble inside at floor level (09:40.0-09:54.1). It is **not** on the east face; the earlier `[INF]` is replaced.
- **Upper hall windows (crash-in targets):** 2-3 tall arched windows (2.4 x 6 m) with diamond leaded glass #9fb6c4 and tracery heads, on tier 2's south and east faces (F08/000010682, 13685; the interior at 07:32.0-07:36.0 shows round-headed arched windows with tracery).
- **Warehouse wing** (owner ref; F08/000028193, F08/000029330, F08/000013435).

The Attack Titan gets the hero rig. The tackle, the swarm and the collapse skid are keyed root motion with dust card bursts.
- **Steam:** 1 ribbon-card material (scrolling noise × gradient alpha, Dithered render method) instanced 3-4 per corpse. Volley flashes are emissive cards visible for 2 frames. Smoke puffs and the beige collapse dust are 1 flipbook each.
