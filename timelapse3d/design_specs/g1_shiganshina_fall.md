## 2. Layout and geography (top-down)

### District shape
- The district is a semicircular, D-shaped bulge on the straight Wall Maria line.
  - Evidence: the ep02 05:44 graphic `ep02/000008258.jpg`, ep01 00:59 `000001313.jpg`, and ep02 13:44 `000019762.jpg` (the district from the north, very dense, a tower in the centre, buttresses facing outward).
- The ep02 05:44.4 graphic (`000008258.jpg`) is a U: a half circle on short straight legs about 0.3–0.4 × the radius long (depth ≈ 0.65–0.7 × width).
- **Current:** a stadium shape, with straight sides of 430 m plus a 290 m radius (`scene_overview_v8.png`).
- **Target [I]:** shorten the straight sides to about 90–130 m so that it reads as the anime's U/D. This is a large change, so it is **P2 and needs the owner**. WARNING: every coordinate in this spec (and in g2: `OUTER_GATE` y −720, `SEAM_IN`, the camera keys) is ABSOLUTE and assumes OG at (0, −720). Shortening the sides moves OG to about y −400 and invalidates all of them; it would have to be done for g1 and g2 together.

### Outer gate (OG): (0, −720), facing south
- The gate is a full-height recessed lift shaft about 10–14 m wide, set between two pilasters (the bay is about as wide as, or slightly narrower than, its neighbours), with a lintel box at the top of the recess. [E] ep02 05:12.7 `000007498.jpg`, ep01 20:40.7 `000029747.jpg`
- Inside face: a brick plinth block about 25 m wide and 10–12 m high, with a cream stone cornice and a low upper storey with small square windows. It carries a central crest pier (a goddess head on a shield) and two barred arched doors, each about 3 × 6 m. [E] `ep01/000027042.jpg`
- Wall top above the gate: between two tower-like pilasters that rise above it, a block with slit windows over a band of small slots, with a large low dome (about 10–12 m across, bronze-brown) behind it. Footprint about 24 × 10 m, [I]. [E] `000027674.jpg`
- After the kick, the opening is a ragged, roughly rectangular hole (broken, jagged top edge, not a neat round arch) AT GROUND LEVEL that fills the width of the gate recess. Measured against the 50 m wall it is about **14–15 m wide and 16–20 m high** (27530: ≈14 × 17 m; 7498: the hole fills the recess bay and its top is at about the lower string course). It reads DARK inside. The wall and the gate-top block above it are intact. `states845.OUTER_HOLE` (half width 8.6, height 25 → 17 × 25 m) is a little too big: use about half width 7.5 and height 18–19, which still matches scene.py's gate opening (14 × 20) and lets the 10–15 m titans through (large ones stoop).   - Evidence [E]: `000000792.jpg`, `000026599.jpg`, `000026970.jpg`.

- **Houses:** 8–10 archetype meshes as collection instances. Roof tint via an object-info random attribute. Merge distant blocks.
- **Titans:** 4–5 base pure-titan rigs with 3 actions each (walk, stoop, idle). Linked duplicates with time offsets and NLA strips. LOD below 0.5 beyond 250 m. Every armature-deformed titan costs CPU evaluation per frame: keep pour titans at about 3–6k triangles each, and keep no more than about 30 deforming titans in the scene at once (hide ones that are off camera).
- **Crowds:** a geometry-nodes point instancer, or camera-facing cards beyond 150 m. Caution: every GN instance of one animated mesh shows the SAME pose on a given frame. To vary the gait, bake each walker's loop to about 8 posed meshes (a pose "flipbook" collection) and pick the pose per instance with (frame + per-point offset) mod 8. Do not give hundreds of people their own armatures.
- **Steam, dust and smoke:** billboard cards with noise textures and flipbook offsets. In Blender 5.x Eevee, use the "Dithered" render method for most cards (no sorting problems with many overlapping cards) and "Blended" only for a few hero cards. Use at most one Eevee volume (the kick dust dome), only during 13–17 s, at a coarse volume resolution; otherwise use 20–30 large shaded cards.
- **Debris:** about 15 hero rocks (rough rounded lumps, not cubes) with hand-keyed ballistic arcs and trail cards. The small debris is a particle system.
- **Lightning:** an emission curve mesh, plus world and sun colour keyframes for the green-gold tint, plus compositor glare (Eevee no longer has a bloom setting).
- **Camera:** feed section 7's keys into the existing `render_fast._Channel`; the peak speeds and yaw rates quoted there come from that same algorithm.
