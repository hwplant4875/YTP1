# EP1 plan v2: the 3D timelapse cut (user, 2026-10-24). Supersedes EP1_must_have_beats.md

## What the user asked for
- "Leave out things like Sasha's potato and the small talk; focus on the 3D timelapse."
- "Graphics like the 250,000 sent out are what matters: shown as if from outside the anime, organising the scenes."
- "Looking from above is for the same reason: we can't do full close-ups."
- "Too much unnecessary stuff crept in; the earlier plan was better."

## Rules
1. The video is a **timelapse of the world**, seen from above: places, armies, walls, titans, territory, numbers. It is not a retelling of dialogue scenes. Every shot is a sky view (60-400 m, or the island map). Characters are tiny, faceless and read only by colour and outfit.
2. Story beats show up as **events in the world**:
   - a gate breaks;
   - a column marches out;
   - smoke colours a formation;
   - a district burns;
   - a titan shifts.
3. **Data graphics on the map are first-class shots**:
   - population counters;
   - territory lost (the Wall Maria belt turning grey);
   - the 250,000 column with a counter running down to ~100;
   - casualty counts, routes and distances;
   - the formation diagram;
   - cadet numbers (about 200 → 20).
   The HUD (Ditty / Pirata One) carries year, place, title and one caption line.
4. Keep the **emotional anchors the user named**, as short sky-view holds with room for the user's anime-clip and voice overlay (see `EP1_user_notes.md`):
   - Yamero (about 2 s on Eren's reaching arm)
   - the oath (about 3 s)
   - Grisha's injection (glitch transition)
   - Marco
   - "It's been five years"
   - Pyxis
   - Erwin's ceremony with the salute
   - Petra
   - "It's your fault"
   - "I'm free"
5. **Cut** everything else that is personal or conversational: Sasha's potato, the balance rig, sparring, the mess hall, the night under the stars, drunk guards, Armin bullied, the family dinner, the trial details, cleaning the castle, the gear inspection, Annie and Armin's talk, the Stohess convoy/disguise/alley scenes, Hitch and Marlo, and similar.
