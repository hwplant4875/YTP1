Shorts Uploader1

Shorts Uploader1 is a private publishing tool used by its owner to upload and manage YouTube Shorts on the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public. It signs in only with the owner's Google accounts, uploads videos the owner has prepared, sets their titles, descriptions and captions, and reads channel statistics so the owner can plan new videos.
