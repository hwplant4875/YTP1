Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Shorts Uploader1 ("the app") uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Your YouTube channel's basic information, videos, captions and statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
  • Permission to upload videos, edit video details and upload captions on your channel.

How it is used

The data is used only to upload and manage videos on your own channel and to show you how those videos perform. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

An OAuth refresh token is stored in the owner's private environment settings so uploads can run on a schedule. Channel statistics may be kept in private working files for planning. No data is stored on a public server.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account permissions or through Google security settings. Stored data is deleted on request to the contact below.
